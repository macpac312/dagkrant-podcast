Australia issues sanctions targeting Israeli settlers

Australia has imposed sanctions on three Israeli individuals and four organisations for illegal settlement expansion in the Occupied West Bank. The targeted sanctions fall short of a wider ranging trade ban imposed by the UK and other allied European nations.

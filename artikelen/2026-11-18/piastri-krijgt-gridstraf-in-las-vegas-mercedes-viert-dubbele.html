Oscar Piastri set for grid penalty at F1 Las Vegas GP

McLaren driver Oscar Piastri is set for a 10-place grid drop at F1's Las Vegas GP for exceeding his engine allowance for the season. Piastri was already on the limit of four internal combustion engines for the year, but will need a fifth Mercedes V6 engine to finish the 2026 season. As it is the first time he is exceeding his ICE allowance, the Australian will be docked 10 places on Las Vegas' starting grid. "Oscar will take a new Internal Combustion Engine in Las Vegas after a reliability issue," the team said in its event preview. "As it is his fifth of the season, he will take a 10-place grid penalty, but he has a good chance to recover positions with overtaking in Saturday's race." McLaren also confirmed it will take Mercedes' upgraded turbocharger, which the manufacturer developed under the FIA's so-called ADUO catch-up mechanism. "Both cars, as with the other HPP-powered cars, will be fitted with a new ADUO-enabled turbo specification: the expected performance benefit is relatively small," McLaren noted.

The Mercedes works team will trial the new turbo on newly crowned world champion Kimi Antonelli's car, while George Russell will stay on the original specification to avoid a grid drop, as Russell had already reached the limit of his allowance. "We will have the ADUO enabled turbo specification available from this weekend," said Mercedes F1 chief Toto Wolff. "This is primarily about proving out the development in race-weekend conditions, with the expected performance benefit relatively small.

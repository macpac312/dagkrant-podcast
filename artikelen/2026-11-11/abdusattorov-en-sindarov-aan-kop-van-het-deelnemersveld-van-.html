Abdusattorov, Sindarov Top Tata Steel Chess 2027 Field

Defending Champion GM Nodirbek Abdusattorov and GM Javokhir Sindarov, potentially the new world champion after the 2026 FIDE World Championship, top the field for Tata Steel Chess 2027. The fields have been announced for the Masters and Challengers, which run from January 15 to January 31, 2027. The Masters section also includes past winners GMs Levon Aronian (four times!), Jorden van Foreest (2021), and Anish Giri(2023).

Masters Section

The past champions are joined by 2750+ rated GMs Vincent Keymer and Alireza Firouzja. The field also includes two teenage prodigies, GM Yagiz Kaan Erdogmus, a 15-year-old already ranked 21st in the world. Slightly older than Erdogmus, 16-year-old GM Andy Woodward graduates to join the masters after winning the Challengers section last year.

GM Hans Niemann returns to the field after a difficult U.S. Championship appearance. He will have an opportunity to level the score against GM Frederik Svane, who defeated him in a memorable game in the recent Chess Olympiad. GM Nihal Sarin would also like to put a difficult Olympiad behind him. GM Amin Tabatabaei has been moving up the ranks this year, and currently sits at 18th in the world.

Finally, there's one unnamed player in the Masters section. You can expect a strong GM to take that final spot.

Challengers Section

The Challengers section is always a strong tournament, with the top finisher joining next year's Masters tournament.

The Challengers section has a strong young field.

This list of stars includes Olympiad gold medalists (Madaminov, Lee, and Samunenkov), Women's Candidates participants (Zhu and Assaubayeva), a former 2750-rated player (Aravindh), and a host of young prodigies.

The 89th edition of Tata Steel Chess takes place January 15-31, 2027, in Wijk aan Zee, the Netherlands. Both the Masters and Challengers groups are 14-player round-robin tournaments. The time control is 120 minutes for 40 moves followed by 30 minutes to the end of the game, with a 30-second increment only from move 41. No draw offers were allowed before move 40.

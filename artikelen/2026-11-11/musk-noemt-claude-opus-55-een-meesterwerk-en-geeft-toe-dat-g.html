# Elon Musk Calls Claude Opus 5.5 a 'Masterpiece', Admits Grok 4.8 Won't Match It

## Elon Musk says the incoming Grok 4.8 will trail Anthropic’s Claude Opus 5.5, praising his rival’s model while predicting Grok 5 could surpass it in two or three months.

* Musk said Grok 4.8 would not be as good as Claude Opus 5.5, calling Anthropic’s model a “masterpiece.” * He said Grok 4.8 has finished pretraining and could begin a limited rollout this week after a rewrite of xAI’s software stack. * Musk predicted Grok 5 could surpass Opus 5.5 in two or three months, but offered no comparison scores or firm release date.

Elon Musk has given a rival AI model an unusually enthusiastic endorsement while acknowledging that his own next release won't be able to beat it. Asked how Grok 4.8 would compare with Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5, the xAI founder answered it would be “not as good.”

“Opus 5.5 is a masterpiece,” Musk posted on X.

The admission came in a [reply on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2120099798078656909) Tuesday to a question from a [user named Dimdv](https://x.com/Dimdv99/status/2120097824851570997). Musk added that Grok 5, a next generation model that will be released after this update, “should exceed Opus 5.5,” potentially in two or three months, although he gave further details for that prediction or a firm date for its release—and he’s known for not sticking to his own target dates.

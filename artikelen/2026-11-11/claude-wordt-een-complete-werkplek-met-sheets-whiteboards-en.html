Anthropic turns Claude into a full workspace with Plugins, spreadsheets, whiteboards, and soon Meeting notes

Anthropic is adding several collaboration features to the Claude app. Claude Sheets lets teams create and edit spreadsheets with AI assistance, including formulas and multiple tabs. Claude Whiteboards lets teams brainstorm ideas and have Claude summarize them. Claude Data (formerly Dashboards) connects to tools like Snowflake and Salesforce to build dashboards and data visualizations.

Anthropic is also launching Interactive Connectors, letting partners like Adobe, Atlassian, and Salesforce embed their apps into Claude, similar to ChatGPT's plugins. Anthropic also says Claude Meetings, which can join calls and take notes, is launching "soon." Enterprise customers get security controls including audit logs, isolated virtual machines, and a compliance API.

PRA publishes second policy statement in relation to amendments to the Large Exposures Framework

On 10 November 2026, the Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA) published its second policy statement in relation to amendments to the Large Exposures (LE) framework (PS20/26).

In CP14/24, the PRA consulted on amendments to the LE framework, taking into consideration internationally agreed Basel standards and their implementation across jurisdictions. The proposals in CP14/24 included the calculation of exposure values for securities financing transactions, the treatment of trading book exposures, intragroup exposure limits and the application of the mandatory substitution approach. PS14/25 published on 17 July 2025, finalised several lower-impact and less complex proposals. PS20/26 sets out the PRA’s final policy following consideration of the responses to the consultation.

The PRA sets out the following in response to feedback on the proposals in CP14/24, in particular:

The changes to the PRA Rulebook and related policy materials set out in PS20/26 will take effect on 1 January 2028. To facilitate orderly implementation, firms may apply for the new NCLEG permission or higher NCLEG permission from 1 July 2027.

The PRA sets out that it is also continuing to assess several consequential LE framework changes arising from the implementation of Basel 3.1, including the treatment of certain off-balance sheet items and other areas affected by changes to the underlying credit risk framework. The PRA will consider these issues separately next year.

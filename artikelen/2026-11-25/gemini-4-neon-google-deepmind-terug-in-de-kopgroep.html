VP ≠ VNP, posted by two labs: An Anthropic GitHub repo reportedly contains a proof by an internal Claude research model that VP ≠ VNP. This is the algebraic analogue of P vs NP, posed by Valiant in 1979, and implies that polynomials such as the permanent lack small circuits over every field (@kimmonismus, repo pointer).

OpenAI's counter-release: OpenAI then posted its own proof and says it was "obtained on November 6, 2026." Neither lab named a model or published an announcement (@AndrewCurran_).

Scope: The result does not imply P ≠ NP, which still requires general Boolean circuit lower bounds (@ZhihuFrontier).

Speculation: Curran argues that both labs are sitting on unreleased results and repeats unconfirmed rumors of cryptography breakthroughs. Treat this as opinion.

Riemann proof clarifications: Levent Alpöge says the "250,000+ pages" claim was a joke. He released only the Lean code because he could not produce a readable writeup, and called it "full of garbage obviously, but compiles" (@kimmonismus, @simonw).

Lineage claim: A Zhihu analysis traces Alpöge's key method to a short OpenAI proof excluding Landau–Siegel zeros. This is an attributed inference (@ZhihuFrontier).

GPT-6 protein design, wet-lab validated: OpenAI released a preprint showing GPT-6 models designing new protein shapes and sequences through reasoning and code, without specialized bio models (@OpenAI).

Results: 14 of 107 resequenced proteins showed binding. 128 of 182 redesigned small antibodies and 179 of 272 antibody fragments retained binding (details).

Cost caveat: On a prior DNA task, GPT-6.1 Sol was about 2,000x more expensive per task than a specialized model (@_lewtun).

GPT-6 Cyber and the Linux kernel: In one run, GPT-6 Cyber helped validate 948 Linux kernel bugs that could grant local root and generated patches for maintainer review (@OpenAI).

Discovery beyond verifiable domains: A historian says GPT-6.2 Astra identified the vicomte de Chabot as the artist of an anonymous 1808 Yale sketchbook in 22 minutes. It also surfaced an obscure microfilmed diary whose handwriting and itinerary matched the sketches (@HistoryGPT).

Exoplanet candidates: Amateurs using Codex and Astra searched Kepler and TESS data. One 13.8-day signal was independently confirmed by a SETI astronomer, and 49 TESS candidates were flagged (@OpenAI).

Robotics saturation: GPT-6.2 Astra on Codex scored 97% on RoboDojo-RC Tier 1 across 120 real-robot trials, and RoboDojo is being retired (@robocurve).

New environments: Einsia released RoboUniverse-10K, a set of 10,807 verifiable Isaac Sim tasks built from real manipulation videos. The top frontier score on held-out tasks is 85.8%, while 7 of 11 models score ≤10% (@EinsiaAI).

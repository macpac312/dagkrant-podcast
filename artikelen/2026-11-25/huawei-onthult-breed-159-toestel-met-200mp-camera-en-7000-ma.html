Huawei unveils 15:9 flagship with 200 MP camera and 7,000 mAh battery

The Huawei OSTA O1 Pro is the first smartphone in the brand-new OSTA series, but aside from minor design tweaks and the removal of the ultra-wide-angle camera, its specifications match those of the older Huawei Pura X View. That doesn’t make the smartphone any less unusual, though: Huawei uses a 6.39-inch display with a 15:9 aspect ratio, making the phone unusually short at 14.4 cm but very wide at 8.7 cm.

The water-resistant body is relatively thin and light at 6.68 millimeters thick and 200 grams, even though Huawei has fitted an especially large 7,000 mAh battery that can be charged at 66 watts via USB-C or at 50 watts wirelessly. The wide AMOLED panel has a resolution of 2,232 x 1,320 pixels, a refresh rate of 120 Hz, and a peak HDR brightness of 6,500 nits.

Huawei equips the device with a 200 MP f/1.8 main camera and a 50 MP f/2.6 periscope telephoto camera with OIS and 3.7x optical zoom. While the device lacks an ultrawide camera, a spectral sensor is intended to deliver more natural color reproduction. The Huawei OSTA O1 Pro also features a 50 MP f/2.4 selfie camera and a HiSilicon Kirin 9030S ARM processor.

Pricing and availability

The Huawei OSTA O1 Pro will launch in China first. The base model with 12 GB of RAM and 256 GB of storage costs ¥5,499 ($825), while Huawei is charging ¥8,799 ($1,320) for the top model with 16 GB of RAM and 1 TB of storage. Huawei has not yet confirmed whether it plans to launch the device in the rest of the world.

Huawei Pura X View sales top 750,000 units in China - TechBriefly

Huawei has sold more than 750,000 Pura X View smartphones in China, according to smartphone sales analyst RDobservation.

Some third-party sellers have listed Huawei smartphones above their standard prices amid strong demand and an electronics-industry supply shortage.

Huawei describes the Pura X View as a wide-screen candybar smartphone. The handset resembles the Pura X Max wide foldable model but does not have a folding design.

The Pura X View has a 6.39-inch display with a 2,232-by-1,320-pixel resolution. The screen supports 1.07 billion colors, the P3 wide color gamut and a 120Hz refresh rate.

The display reaches up to 6,000 nits of peak brightness. Huawei lists a 96.1% screen-to-body ratio and 1.05mm bezels on all four sides.

The phone measures 6.68mm thick and weighs 201 grams. It includes a 7,000mAh battery, a 66W wired charger in the box and support for 50W wireless charging.

The Pura X View has a 200-megapixel main camera with an F1.8 aperture and optical image stabilization, an 8-megapixel ultra-wide macro camera with an F2.2 aperture, and a 50-megapixel periscope telephoto camera with an F2.6 aperture and optical image stabilization. Huawei also included a second-generation Red Maple color camera.

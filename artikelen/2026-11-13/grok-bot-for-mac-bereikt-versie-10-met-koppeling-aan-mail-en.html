Grok Bot for Mac adds integration with Apple Mail, Messages, Notes, and more - 9to5Mac

Grok Bot for Mac just gained a bunch of new Apple integrations, including Messages, Mail, Notes, and more.

“Grok Bot on Mac can now use Messages, Mail, Notes, Reminders, Calendar, Location, and Contacts,” per the @bot account on X.

Integrating with these six Apple apps, plus location, should make Grok Bot on the Mac much more powerful for users deep in the Apple ecosystem.

Earlier this year, ChatGPT added its own Messages integration. More recently, its Grok Bot competitor called dot picked up Apple Mail and Calendar integration.

Grok Bot from SpaceX/Cursor arrived in August with an iPhone and Mac app to start. It later expanded to the iPad.

Last month, Grok Bot gained its own email address for agentic work. Using Claude intelligence behind the scenes also made it smarter.

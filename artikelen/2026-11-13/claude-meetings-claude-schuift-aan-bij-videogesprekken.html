Claude Meetings lets Anthropic's AI join your video calls

Anthropic has launched Claude Meetings, a feature that puts Claude into video calls as a visible participant. During a call, Claude can answer questions, generate documents and whiteboards, and log notes and action items, responding in text by default and accessing connected tools when granted permission.

The feature is in beta for Team and Enterprise plans, with Google Meet as the only supported platform so far. Anthropic says more platforms will follow. Organizations running HIPAA or customer-managed encryption configurations can't use Meetings yet.Ad

Claude Meetings extends recently announced capabilities like whiteboards and data dashboards that are gradually reshaping the Claude app into an all-in-one office tool.Ad

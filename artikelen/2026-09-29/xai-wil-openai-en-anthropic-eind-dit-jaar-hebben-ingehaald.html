xAi’s models to catch up OpenAI and Anthropic by year-end ⋆ Electronics Weekly

Elon Musk expects that, by Christmas, xAI’s models will be as advanced as those from Anthropic and Open AI,

“We will keep accelerating,” said Musk, “our AI efforts are only 3 years old, vs 6 and 10 years old for Anthropic and OpenAI. If our second derivative remains strong, SpaceX will reach pole position in about 6 months.”

‘Second derivative’ in Musk-speak means ‘rate of acceleration’.

The key is the Colossus 2 datacentre in Memphis (pictured).

At the moment, the datacentre is running on 110,000 GB200 GPUs and 440,000 GB300 GPUs.

The intention is to add 660,000 GB300s by the end of the year.

By the end of this week, the datacentre should bring 220,000 GB300 GPUs online and have another 220,000 operational by the end of November.

Before the end of the year, Colossus 2should have the final batch of 220,000 GB300 GPUs giving it 1.2 million Blackwell GPUs.

Colossus 2 adds to Colossus 1 which currently has 230,000 GPUs (150k H100 + 50k H200 + 30k GB200).

With a year-end total of 1.44 million GPUs, Musk expects xAI’s models to be as advanced as OpenAI’s and Anthropic’s.

Anthropic rolls out Claude Whiteboards and Sheets, makes Claude Data generally available

Anthropic has introduced Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets in beta and made Claude Dashboards generally available under a new name, Claude Data. The tools add collaborative whiteboards, spreadsheet creation and editing, and company data analysis to Claude.

Claude Whiteboards

Claude Whiteboards is a shared board where users can brainstorm with Claude and collaborate with their teams in real time. Users can talk through a plan with Claude, which can turn the discussion into a board containing a timeline, owners, and dates.

Teams can also use Whiteboards during retrospective meetings, where participants can add notes to a shared board. Claude can group the notes by theme and draft action items for each person. Users can also select a specific section of the board by dragging a box around it and asking Claude questions about its contents.

Claude Sheets

Claude Sheets lets users generate and edit spreadsheets through prompts. It can create spreadsheets with tabs, formulas, formatting, and drop-downs, often from a request for something like a campaign tracker or hiring budget. Claude can also edit spreadsheet cells, outlining each new change so users can track their edits. Sheets can be shared with team members so they can work on them alongside Claude.

Claude Data

Anthropic has renamed Claude Dashboards, which launched in beta in October, to Claude Data and made it generally available. The tool initially allowed teams to ask quick questions about company data stored in services such as Amazon Redshift, BigQuery, and ClickHouse.

Anthropic said the name change reflects how companies use their data beyond dashboards and the product’s broader scope. Certified queries and live sharing are currently in beta.

Claude Data partners include Amplitude, Mixpanel, Omni, PostHog, and Sigma.

Interactive connectors get full-app support

Interactive connectors can now open as full applications alongside a Claude conversation or in full-screen mode. Users can work directly in applications such as Adobe, Atlassian, Box, and Notion, or ask Claude to make changes with them.

Availability

Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets are available in beta, while Claude Data is generally available. All three tools work on the web, desktop, and mobile and are included in all paid Claude plans. Claude Data requires a data connector, such as a data warehouse or CRM, to access company information.

On Team plans, all three tools are enabled by default. On Enterprise plans, Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets are disabled by default, while Claude Data will be enabled by default on November 19, 2026. Administrators can change these settings under Organization settings > Artifacts.

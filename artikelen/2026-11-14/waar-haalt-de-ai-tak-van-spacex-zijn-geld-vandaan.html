SpaceX Cuts Grok Video Team: So Where Does Its $7.6B AI Business Get Its Money?

HTML_TAG_START SpaceX has cut most of the team behind Grok Imagine, its tool that makes AI videos and images. Yet its AI business is booming. HTML_TAG_END

HTML_TAG_START About 60 people lost their jobs in the past week, The Information reported. Only a handful remain. SpaceX has not commented. HTML_TAG_END

HTML_TAG_START AI made $7.6 billion of SpaceX's $14.6 billion in revenue last quarter, up 830% in a year. The Motley Fool credits deals renting computing power to AI developers such as Anthropic and Alphabet, plus rising Grok use. HTML_TAG_END

HTML_TAG_START In the second quarter, AI solutions and infrastructure made $2.19 billion of the AI unit's $2.56 billion. Advertising made $367 million. SpaceX has signed $14.1 billion in cloud deals. HTML_TAG_END

HTML_TAG_START In August, Musk said he expects "maybe 10%" of the company's computing power to go to training Grok. HTML_TAG_END

HTML_TAG_START Anthropic, one of those paying customers, makes Claude. This week, Musk said his Grok 4.8 is "not as good" as Claude Opus 5.5. "Opus 5.5 is a masterpiece," he added, BeInCrypto reported. HTML_TAG_END

HTML_TAG_START In August, SpaceX President Gwynne Shotwell said the company was "rapidly expanding our compute capacity to meet our own needs as Grok expands." Three months later, Grok's video team is mostly gone. HTML_TAG_END

Xiaomi 18 Fold vs Other Foldables: 5 Features Worth Comparing

Xiaomi 18 Fold: The Xiaomi 18 Fold is a wide, book-style foldable with a 7.58-inch inner display, 5.38-inch cover screen, Leica triple cameras, and a 6,000mAh battery. It positions itself as a premium alternative to Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold 8..Display experience: Its inner and outer screens both use 1-120Hz LTPO panels with 4,000-nit peak brightness and Xiaomi HyperRGB technology.

Compared with the Galaxy Z Fold 8, the 18 Fold offers a similarly large main display but a more distinctive 2:1 aspect ratio.Camera system: Xiaomi equips the 18 Fold with a 200MP main camera, 50MP ultrawide, and 50MP 3.5x periscope telephoto lens.

That gives it a clear hardware advantage over foldables such as the Galaxy Z Fold 8, which lacks a dedicated telephoto camera..Battery and charging: The 6,000mAh silicon-carbon battery is substantially larger than the Galaxy Z Fold 8’s 4,800mAh cell, while 67W wired and 50W wireless charging add convenience.

Independent testing also found the Xiaomi lasting longer in Wi-Fi battery tests..Performance and storage: Xiaomi uses its in-house 3nm XRing O3 chip, with up to 16GB RAM and 1TB storage.

The phone also includes Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6.0, USB 3.2 Gen 2, UWB, and an IR blaster, making it a feature-rich flagship foldable..Portability and design: At 219g and 10.68mm folded, the 18 Fold is heavier than the 201g Galaxy Z Fold 8, but its compact folded footprint and metal frame support one-handed use.

Buyers should weigh the larger battery and cameras against the extra weight..Value proposition: The 18 Fold stands out for camera versatility, battery capacity, bright dual displays, and fast charging.

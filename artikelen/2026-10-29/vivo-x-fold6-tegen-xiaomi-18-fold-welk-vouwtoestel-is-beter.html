Vivo X Fold6 and Xiaomi 18 Fold approach the premium foldable market differently, combining flagship performance, versatile cameras, and large inner displays. Vivo focuses on Zeiss-tuned photography, a larger internal screen, and a substantial battery, while Xiaomi counters with Leica optics, Dolby Vision video, and a distinctive compact design. The differences extend to charging, software, and pricing, making this comparison useful for buyers deciding which foldable delivers the better overall experience.

1. Design and Display

Build and Feel

The Vivo X Fold6 combines an aluminum frame with a glass front when folded and a glass-fiber-reinforced plastic back. Its IP58/IP59 rating provides protection against dust and water, making it suitable for users who want a premium foldable without sacrificing durability. The Xiaomi 18 Fold uses Dragon Crystal Glass 3 on its cover, a glass back, and an aluminum frame, with a plastic front when unfolded. It carries the same IP58/IP59 rating. Xiaomi’s reinforced cover glass is appealing, while Vivo’s glass-fiber construction offers a different approach to managing the demands of a foldable design.

Display Quality

Vivo has an advantage in screen real estate with its 8.02-inch inner LTPO AMOLED panel, which supports 120Hz refresh rates, HDR10+, HDR Vivid, and Dolby Vision. Its 6.51-inch cover display also supports 120Hz and boasts a claimed 5,000-nit peak brightness. Xiaomi offers a 7.58-inch inner LTPO AMOLED display with the same major HDR formats and a 120Hz refresh rate. Its 5.38-inch cover screen reaches a claimed 4,000 nits. Vivo’s larger panels make multitasking and reading more comfortable, while Xiaomi’s smaller format may appeal to users who prefer a more compact cover screen.

Vivo X Fold6 wins this round with its larger inner and outer displays, higher claimed cover-screen brightness, and equally strong water and dust resistance.

2. Specifications Including Battery

Performance

The Vivo X Fold6 uses the MediaTek Dimensity 9500 Super, a 3nm chipset paired with a Mali-G1 Ultra MC12 GPU. The Xiaomi 18 Fold runs on Xiaomi’s 3nm XRing O3 processor, featuring a 10-core CPU and Arm G2-Ultra NX MC16 graphics. Both devices offer up to 16GB of RAM and 1TB of UFS 4.1 storage, giving them a strong foundation for demanding applications, multitasking, and large files. However, direct performance comparisons require reliable independent benchmarks, particularly because these chipsets use different architectures. Vivo’s MediaTek platform is an appealing choice, while Xiaomi’s in-house silicon could deliver tighter integration with HyperOS 4.

Battery and Charging

Vivo packs a silicon-carbon battery rated at 6,900mAh or 7,000mAh, depending on the version, with 80W wired and 40W wireless charging. It also supports reverse wired and 5W reverse wireless charging. Xiaomi has a 6,000mAh silicon-carbon battery, 67W wired charging, and faster 50W wireless charging, plus reverse wired charging. Vivo’s larger battery and quicker wired charging should suit users who spend long hours working or travelling. Xiaomi counters with higher wireless charging output, which is convenient for compatible charging accessories. Actual endurance will depend on software optimisation, display usage, and network conditions.

Vivo X Fold6 wins for battery capacity and wired charging speed, while Xiaomi 18 Fold offers faster wireless charging. Vivo is the more practical choice for heavy users.

3. Camera

Main and Secondary Lenses

The Vivo X Fold6 features a 200MP main camera with optical image stabilisation, a 50MP periscope telephoto camera offering 3x optical zoom, and a 50MP ultrawide lens. Zeiss optics, T* lens coating, laser autofocus, and a colour spectrum sensor strengthen its photography credentials. Xiaomi counters with a 200MP main camera, a 50MP telephoto camera with approximately 3x optical zoom, and a 50MP ultrawide lens. Leica lenses, laser autofocus, and a colour spectrum sensor provide a distinct imaging approach. Vivo’s dedicated periscope system is promising for zoom photography, while Xiaomi’s Leica collaboration could appeal to users who prefer its colour science. Both offer flagship-level versatility, but real-world image quality depends on processing and lighting.

Selfie Camera

Vivo equips both the inner and cover displays with 20MP selfie cameras, while Xiaomi uses 16MP cameras on both screens. Vivo’s higher resolution could retain more detail in selfies and video calls, although sensor quality and image processing matter just as much. Both phones support HDR, and their front cameras record at 1080p. Xiaomi offers 1080p recording at up to 60fps, whereas Vivo lists 30fps recording. Xiaomi therefore has an advantage for smoother front-camera video, while Vivo looks stronger on paper for still-image detail. Neither specification alone guarantees superior skin tones or low-light selfies.

Vivo X Fold6 takes the camera round for its periscope hardware, Zeiss optics, and higher-resolution selfie cameras. Xiaomi remains compelling for Leica-inspired imaging and smoother selfie video.

4. Pricing

The Vivo X Fold6 is priced around $1,200 (₹111,000), positioning it as the more affordable option in this premium foldable comparison. The Xiaomi 18 Fold is priced around $1,600 (₹155,000), commanding a considerable premium. That difference gives Vivo a stronger starting position for buyers who want a high-end foldable without moving into the most expensive segment. Both devices offer 200MP main cameras, advanced LTPO AMOLED displays, flagship-oriented processors, and generous storage configurations. However, regional taxes, import costs, availability, and variant differences can affect actual retail prices. Buyers should verify local pricing and warranty coverage before making a purchase, especially if either device is sold through unofficial channels.

Is the price justified?

Xiaomi’s premium may appeal to buyers who specifically want Leica camera tuning, HyperOS 4, Dolby Vision video recording, and 50W wireless charging. Its compact cover-screen approach also provides a different everyday experience. However, Vivo offers a larger inner display, a higher-capacity battery, faster wired charging, and Zeiss-branded camera features for substantially less. Those advantages are more immediately useful to a broad range of foldable users. Xiaomi could justify its higher price through software refinement and imaging performance, but those benefits need real-world testing. On the supplied specifications alone, Vivo provides the more balanced package for the money.

Vivo X Fold6 wins on value, delivering larger displays, a bigger battery, and versatile cameras at a lower price. Xiaomi suits buyers prioritising its specific software and imaging features.

5. Conclusion

Vivo X Fold6 stands out with its 8.02-inch inner display, 6.51-inch cover screen, 200MP main camera, 3x periscope telephoto lens, and battery rated up to 7,000mAh. Its 80W wired charging and Zeiss camera collaboration further strengthen its flagship credentials. The Xiaomi 18 Fold offers its own distinct advantages, including Leica optics, Dolby Vision video recording, 50W wireless charging, Ultra Wideband support, and HyperOS 4. Its Dragon Crystal Glass 3 cover glass also adds to its appeal.

Both devices support 120Hz inner displays, IP58/IP59 protection, and up to 1TB of storage. The deciding factor is how those features translate into everyday use. Vivo’s larger screens and battery favour productivity and extended use, while Xiaomi offers an attractive alternative for buyers who value its camera partnership and software ecosystem.

Overall winner: Vivo X Fold6. It offers the stronger all-round package with larger displays, a bigger battery, faster wired charging, versatile camera hardware, and a significantly lower starting price. Xiaomi 18 Fold remains a capable alternative for Leica photography enthusiasts and users who prioritise faster wireless charging, Dolby Vision video, and HyperOS 4.

However, based on the supplied specifications and pricing, Vivo delivers more practical flagship features for the money. Unless Xiaomi’s software experience or camera processing proves substantially better in independent testing, the Vivo X Fold6 is the more sensible choice for most premium foldable buyers.

Huawei Pura 90 Plus unveiled with Kirin 9025, 100 W charging and a starting price of $815

The Huawei Pura 90 Plus has a streamlined body with a curved metal frame and a rounded back for a comfortable grip. Display The Huawei Pura 90 Plus features a 6.8-inch OLED display with a resolution of 2848 × 1276 pixels.

LTPO technology lets the refresh rate vary from 1 to 120 Hz, depending on the content being displayed. Peak brightness reaches 3,000 nits. The screen is protected by second-generation Kunlun Glass.

Hardware The phone is powered by Huawei’s Kirin 9025 chip. Huawei says that optimizing the hardware and software has boosted the device’s overall performance by 19% compared with the Pura 80 Pro.

The phone runs HarmonyOS 7.0 and is available with 12 or 16 GB of RAM and up to 1 TB of storage. Cameras The rear camera system consists of four cameras: 50 MP main camera: variable f/1.6-4.0 aperture, 22.5 mm equivalent focal length, optical image stabilization (OIS). 40 MP ultra-wide camera: f/2.2 aperture, 13 mm equivalent focal length. 48 MP periscope camera: f/2.1 aperture, OIS, 48 optical zoom, 92.5 mm equivalent focal length, and macro photography from as close as 5 cm. 1.5 MP spectral camera.

A 13 MP selfie camera with an f/2.0 aperture sits in a centered punch-hole at the top of the display. It supports AI features, HDR, autofocus, 4K video recording, and slow-motion recording at 240 frames per second in 1080p.

Battery The battery has a capacity of 5,700 mAh. The phone supports 100 W wired charging, 80 W wireless charging, and 18 W reverse charging. Additional features The phone supports satellite messaging via the BeiDou system, as well as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 6, NFC, and GPS.

The fingerprint scanner is built into the power button. The body is rated IP68 and IP69 for water and dust resistance. The device measures 163 × 76,1 × 8,3 mm and weighs 220 grams.

Release date, colors and prices Sales of the Huawei Pura 90 Plus in China start on October 31. The phone will be available in four colors: black, white, purple, and gold. Prices: 12 GB of RAM and 256 GB of storage – $815; 12 GB of RAM and 512 GB of storage – $889; 16 GB of RAM and 1 TB of storage – $1037.

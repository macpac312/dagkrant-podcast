Launch: Google DeepMind announced Gemini 4 Carbon two weeks after Argon. It is positioned as state of the art on cybersecurity and long-horizon tasks (@sundarpichai, @GoogleDeepMind).

Claimed cyber results: 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro for vulnerability finding, 54.8% on ExploitGym for exploitation and 71.0% on CWE-bench for patching (@sundarpichai).

Availability: Carbon first goes to trusted cyber defenders through the Fairwind Program, with wider developer access promised "as soon as possible" (@GoogleAI).

Pricing: Phil Schmid quotes $2/$10 per million input/output tokens with an asterisk (@_philschmid). Artificial Analysis lists $4/$20 standard pricing with a 50% launch discount that has no end date. Cached input costs $0.20 per million tokens (@ArtificialAnlys).

Specs: The model has a 1M-token context window and 1M max output. Inputs are text, image, video and speech; output is text (@ValsAI).

Safety claims (Google): Successful cheating on Terminal-Bench 4.0 fell to 0.0%, and cheating attempts fell to 0.4%. Unsupported claims are down about 2x versus Argon. Overreach fell 26% and under-service fell 29% (@OfficialLoganK).

Artificial Analysis: Carbon is #1 on Intelligence Index v5 with a score of 59. It leads 4 of the 9 index evals: HLE-Diamond, TB-Science, AA-Omniscience and AutomationBench (@ArtificialAnlys).

Coding: On the Coding Agent Index, Carbon scores 59 using Antigravity CLI, versus 62 for Claude Code with Opus 5.5 [max]. It scores 51% on FrontierCode v1.1, versus 56% for Opus.

Knowledge work: Carbon reaches 1609 Elo on AA-Briefcase, up 126 points from Argon. It still trails Grok 4.7, Muse Wonder and Kimi K3.1 Pro (1678).

Cost: Carbon costs $7.76 per index task, versus $10.04 for Opus 5.5 and $6.25 for GPT-6 Astra. It uses 123k output tokens per task, 29% fewer than Opus.

Hallucination: Carbon leads Omniscience with 54. Its hallucination rate rose from Argon's 4% to 11%, which is still the second lowest measured. Opus 5.5 and Astra are both at 50%.

Arena: Carbon is #1 in Text Arena (1528), #2 in Agent Arena at a $1.80 median cost per task, and #4 in Code Arena: WebDev (1768) (@arena). Arena attributes the gains over Argon to more praise, more confirmed successes and better steerability (@arena).

Vals: Carbon is #2 on the Vals Index and #1 on the Finance Index and Legal Research Benchmark. It produces 64% more output tokens than Argon, mostly on finance, tax and legal tasks (@ValsAI, tokens).

Coding benchmarks: Carbon scores 61.2% on FrontierSWE, behind GPT-6 Astra (65.5), Opus 5.5 (62.3) and Sonnet 5.5 (61.9) (@ProximalHQ). It sets a narrow state of the art of 9.6% on SWE-sweep (@KLieret).

Andon Labs: Carbon is #1 on Vending-Bench 2 (@andonlabs). It is also #1 on Blueprint-Bench 2, where it ties the human baseline for 3D spatial understanding (@andonlabs).

Harness: The coding-agent results were measured in Antigravity CLI, so results in other harnesses are untested (@Yuchenj_UW).

Hallucination risk: Some users are concerned about hallucination in cyber use (@scaling01).

Lab gap: scaling01 thinks Google remains about two months behind the next OpenAI and Anthropic releases (@scaling01).

RSI framing: On the earnings call, Sundar Pichai said AI is increasing training and launch velocity. He expects "continued acceleration because of RSI" over the next twelve months (@AndrewCurran_).

Unconfirmed: Claims of a stronger unreleased Gemini model are not confirmed (@kimmonismus).

Internal workflow: A Gemini engineer describes overnight agent loops and swarms that inspect RL data. In that workflow, launches now feel like "periodic checkpoints" (@zhangqiaorjc).

AA Intelligence Index v5: The rebuilt index runs 60% on private tests (@TheRundownAI).

Ranking before Carbon: Opus 5.5 scored 58, followed by GPT-6 Astra (57), GPT-6.1 Sol (55), Sonnet 5.5 (54) and GPT-6.1 Terra (51).

Open models: Kimi K3.1 Pro fell from 55 to 46. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and GLM-5.3 both score 32, below Claude Haiku 5.5 at 36.

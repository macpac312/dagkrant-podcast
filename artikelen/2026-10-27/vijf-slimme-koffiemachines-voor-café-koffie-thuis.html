From cappuccino to latte: 5 smart coffee makers that bring café-style drinks home

The coffee culture in India has evolved significantly in the past decade. People not longer just want espresso or home-style coffee, they also want other drink such as mocha, cappuccino and cafe latte, in the comfort of their homes without having to step out every time.

So how do you get your perfect cup of coffee at home? Enter: a coffee maker.Coffee makers aren't exactly a new appliance. They have been available in Indian market for over a decade.

But there is a huge difference in the models available in the market these days. First, there is coffee maker available almost everyone. The features and range of drinks that these machines can make obviously differs from machine to machine, which varies as per the pricing.

Second, this diversity in the availability of coffee machines has given users greater control over the kind of drink that they want and the way they want their the coffee to be brewed.High-end coffee machines these days come with features such as automatic milk frothing, built-in grinders, customisable brewing settings and app-based controls to prepare drinks that suit your taste.For those who enjoy café favourites but want more control over their coffee routine, investing in the right machine could be worth considering.

Some coffee makers simplify the process with one-touch drink preparation, while others let you adjust coffee strength, serving size and milk texture.

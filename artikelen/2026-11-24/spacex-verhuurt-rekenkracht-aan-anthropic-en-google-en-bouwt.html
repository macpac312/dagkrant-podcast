Anthropic's appetite for compute is pushing xAI toward a rent-over-build strategy

Anthropic's massive compute needs align with growing profit pressure at xAI, Elon Musk's AI unit. xAI has rented compute to rivals since May, and its June IPO made the push for revenue even more urgent, the New York Times reports. Anthropic is now xAI's biggest customer with a contract worth roughly $3.1 billion a month. Google spends $800 million. Either deal can be canceled with 90 days' notice.

xAI now accounts for over half of xAI's total revenue. Insiders say the company had to finish new data centers by October to fill Anthropic's orders, and Musk says the Claude maker wants to rent even more.

In August, Musk pushed engineers in a group chat for "revenue per GPU," frustrated in part that xAI's own models can barely compete. He reportedly renamed an internal chat "Sell compute because we can't compete."Ad

xAI is cutting or restructuring its own AI projects to match that shift. Unit chief Mike Nicolls told staff in November that the 60-person team behind the Grok Imagine image and video generator would be disbanded. An internal memo pointed to critical business priorities. xAI shut down its AI avatars in September, and OpenAI also killed its Sora video model over capacity limits.Ad

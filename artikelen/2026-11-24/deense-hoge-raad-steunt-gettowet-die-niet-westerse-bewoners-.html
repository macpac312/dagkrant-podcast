Danish court backs ‘ghetto law’ used to evict ‘non-Western’ residents

Denmark’s Supreme Court has ruled against the ‘non-Western’ tenants evicted from Mjolnerparken housing estate under the country’s so-called ‘ghetto law’. It allows for the relocation of residents from minority-heavy areas, which the government says will promote integration.

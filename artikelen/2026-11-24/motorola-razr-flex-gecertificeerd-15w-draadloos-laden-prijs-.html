Motorola Razr Flex gets certified, wireless charging speed revealed

Motorola's first wide foldable, the Razr Flex, has been leaking a lot lately, most recently in renders showing off both of its colors. Now, the device has been certified by the Wireless Power Consortium (WPC), and the listing reveals how fast it will charge wirelessly.

The listing is dated November 22, and it's for the model number XT2761, which is the same one that showed up in a TrustCB certification last week. According to the WPC, the Razr Flex supports the Extended Power Profile (EPP) with a maximum load power of 15W. That's lower than the 25W wireless charging tipped by past leaks, though it doesn't necessarily rule that out, since phones can get faster speeds through other profiles or proprietary standards.

For some reason, the image attached to the listing shows a traditional clamshell Razr with a tall cover screen, not the short and wide book-style design we've seen in the leaks. So it's most likely just a placeholder.

The Razr Flex is rumored to come with a 6.92-inch folding screen with a 165Hz refresh rate, a 4.97-inch cover display, the as-yet-unannounced SM8845 Pro chipset, a 50MP main camera, a 50MP ultrawide, a 5,000 mAh battery, and Android 17. Earlier leaks pointed to a mid-December launch, but Motorola's own support site has hinted at a 2027 debut.

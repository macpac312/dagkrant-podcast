“I’m not going to remove the repository”

There is a hacking tool called ARTEX that is causing havoc around the world. It lets a hacker plug in their LLM of choice, and ARTEX will then spin up a bunch of AI subagents focused on different aspects of hacking, such as scoping out a target or stealing data. This tool can complete an entire hack of a target in just a few hours. The original author of ARTEX took down the tool in October after it became clear that bad people were using it. But, as I write below, ARTEX is free. Several people are still distributing it on GitHub. I spoke to them about it, asked them why, and for their thoughts about requests to take the tool down.

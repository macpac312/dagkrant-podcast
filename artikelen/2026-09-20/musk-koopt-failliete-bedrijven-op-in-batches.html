Why Does Elon Musk Want to Buy Data from Defunct Companies

So why did Elon Musk suddenly set his sights on this batch of data? Is it really just a random flash of inspiration?

This is more like a necessary missing puzzle piece that must be completed after Grok has gone through multiple rounds of training.

Before coming up with this unorthodox solution, Grok was mainly trained on two types of data.

The first type is data from X (formerly Twitter), which has shaped the core "personality" of Grok.

X has massive real-time content, hot debates, memes, sarcastic remarks and all kinds of fragmented expressions.

It catches references fast, talks a lot, dares to push back, and more or less carries Elon Musk's personal traits.

The second type of data comes from AI tutors.

This team is mainly composed of domain experts and labeling staff, who are responsible for equipping Grok with full professional capabilities.

Where there are gaps in finance, science and code, they will fill in the corresponding data.

But this team has been undergoing adjustments recently.

According to Bloomberg's report in June, xAI once suspended the recruitment of AI tutors, and later replaced the head of this team.

The previous team leader was Diego Pasini.

Business Insider once mentioned that he entered the University of Pennsylvania after graduating from high school in 2023, then dropped out to join xAI, and participated in the management of Grok's data labeling team.

His profile picture is in sci-fi mecha style, the background is a picture of classical ruins, and his bio only has one line: "In pursuit of truth".

He didn't finish college following the traditional path, but already got the chance to train Grok first.

His life experience and personal style are exactly what Elon Musk likes (doge).

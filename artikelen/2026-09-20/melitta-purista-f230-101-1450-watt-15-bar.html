Melitta Purista F230-101 - 1,450 W and 15 Bar

Melitta Purista F230-101 pairs a 1.2 L water tank with a 125 g bean hopper and 15 bar pressure. Melitta positions the model in the coffee machine segment.

Melitta Purista F230-101 puts a 1.2 L water tank, a 125 g bean hopper, and 15 bar pressure in one compact automatic coffee machine. Its model name appears across retailer and review listings that also tie it to coffee and espresso preparation.

Core Specifications

Independent listings describe the machine with a 1,450 W power draw, a 15 bar pump, and a conical burr grinder. The same model is listed with a 20 cm width, a 45.5 cm depth, and a 32.5 cm height.

Test coverage also points to a two-cup function, removable brew group, and a 15-step grinder setting. That combination keeps the feature set focused on black coffee and espresso rather than milk drinks.

Brand And US Presence

Melitta's North America headquarters is in Clearwater, Florida, and the company says it serves customers across the US and Canada there. That places the brand's US operations in a documented domestic office rather than a foreign mailing address.

The Purista line is listed as a bean-to-cup coffee machine with no milk frother on a comparison page, and a Dutch consumer site describes it as a fully automatic machine for bean coffee only. The model's profile stays centered on espresso and coffee, with no milk-system detour.

Price And Positioning

Price listings in Europe place the Melitta Purista F230-101 around 369.00 EUR to 602.11 EUR, with one comparison page showing a 369.00 EUR floor and another test page listing 335.99 EUR. Those listings show where the machine sits in the market, but they do not establish a US dollar street price.

For buyers who want a bean-to-cup machine with a compact footprint, a 1.2 L tank, and a 125 g bean hopper, the model's appeal is plain. The limits are just as plain: no milk system and a fixed focus on black coffee styles.

Verdict For Coffee Buyers

Melitta Purista F230-101 fits readers who want an automatic coffee machine with a grinder, two-cup brewing, and a stripped-back feature set. The model is narrow in scope, and that narrow scope is the point.

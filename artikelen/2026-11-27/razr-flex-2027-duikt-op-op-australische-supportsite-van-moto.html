Motorola Razr Flex 2027: Snapdragon Chip & Expected Price! - Times Bull

The Motorola Razr Flex 2027 has appeared on Motorola's Australian support website, hinting at a possible upcoming foldable smartphone. Leaks suggest a wide clamshell design with a 6.92-inch 165Hz inner display, Snapdragon processor, 5,000mAh battery and dual 50MP cameras

Ankit Kumar

It appears that the Motorola Razr Flex 2027 is very real, as it has been added to Motorola’s support site. However, as the launch date, specs, and price have not been officially announced, and there is conflicting information on when it will be released, the information below should be considered pre-release.

The only clue we have comes from Motorola’s support site in Australia. The phone can be seen in the list of Motorola products, between the Razr 70 Ultra and Razr Fold. In the description we are given a release date of August 2024, Android 16, and security updates until August 2029. It seems likely that some of these dates have just been filled in as placeholders, and shouldn’t be taken as set in stone. It can also be seen in Motorola’s security updates selection tool. We have been given no information on design or specs in these pages.

Render leaks show that the Motorola Razr Flex 2027 is going to be a wide clamshell device, similar to the rumoured format of the Apple iPhone Duo. The external screen measures around 4.97 inches, while the internal screen measures around 6.92 inches. This is wider than a standard phone, but less than the 8.1 inch screen in the Razr Fold. The colour options shown in the renders include a dark green (Dark Botanical Garden) and a brown (Coriander). The back designs are weave and faux leather. The internal screen has a high refresh rate of 165Hz.

We have a lot of information from previous leaks and tipsters. It is expected to have a Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chip, 12GB of RAM, a 5,000mAh battery with 25W wireless charging and two 50MP rear cameras. Some sources suggest that this foldable could have IP48 and IP49 ratings, which is unusual for a foldable. However, in another leak, it was stated that the phone uses an unauncleared Snapdragon chip, meaning the chip is unsure.

One European retailer leak states that the price of the 12GB/256GB model will be about €1510-€1595 ($1700-$1800). Multiple other reports have given a similar price, and this places this phone cheaper than the Galaxy Z Fold 8 and the Razr Fold. As no price has been confirmed by Motorola, this is just speculation.

Previous reports have suggested this phone may be released in December 2026, however, a few sites now suggest otherwise. The recent addition to the support site in Australia suggests that it may be released in 2027. It may be showcased at CES in January. However, without confirmation from Motorola, it is possible that it could be released in both 2026 and 2027.

This wide-foldable phone does seem worth keeping an eye out for, however, if the price is lower than the Galaxy Z Fold 8, then it could be a good option. However, do not make any plans to purchase this phone based on the above specs/prices. Wait for an official announcement from Motorola before making a decision.

Huawei's HarmonyOS Surpasses 100 Million Installations | Silicon

Huawei’s HarmonyOS has surpassed 100 million installations across smartphones, tablets and PCs, the company said, in what it called a “landmark moment” for the self-developed operating system.

The total was reached this month for HarmonyOS versions 5, 6 and 7, said Huawei.

HarmonyOS 5, also marketed as HarmonyOS Next, was rolled out in October 2024 as the first version to remove app compatibility with Google’s Android, amid a push by Huawei to develop an independent app ecosystem.

‘Critical scale threshold’

HarmonyOS now counts more than 460,000 apps and services from 12 million registered developers, Huawei said, including widely used apps like Tencent’s WeChat, which arrived on the platform in January of last year.

Richard Yu Chengdong, chair of Huawei’s Consumer Business Group, said in a social media post that the threshold was a “landmark moment” and that the pool of apps had reached a “critical scale threshold”.

Domestic market share

“It has officially entered a new phase of maturity, usability and innovation-driven growth,” Yu wrote.

HarmonyOS was the second-biggest smartphone operating system in China in the second quarter of this year, with 24 percent of the market, after Android’s 58 percent and ahead of Apple’s iOS with 18 percent, according to Counterpoint Research.

Huawei launched HarmonyOS initially in August 2019 after US sanctions cut off access to US technologies including Android.

Grok Is the Tenant That Stays

HTML_TAG_START On SpaceX's third-quarter call, management said it aims to split its compute roughly 50-50 between its own use and rentals, give or take 10 points. Capital spending jumped from $4.4 billion in 2023 to $20.9 billion in 2025: HTML_TAG_END

HTML_TAG_START This year it's far higher: $48.1 billion in the first nine months, with $35 billion to $40 billion more guided for the fourth quarter. Those chips lose value rented or idle. If Anthropic hands back those H200s, a fast-growing Grok could use them while SpaceX finds a new tenant, though SpaceX rented out Colossus 1 after Grok struggled with its mixed chips. HTML_TAG_END

Huang Has Every Reason to Cheer

HTML_TAG_START Huang is talking up a big customer, of course, and gave no number for Grok Bot's usage. Nvidia's data center revenue rose 110% to $107.6 billion in its fiscal 2027 third quarter. HTML_TAG_END

HTML_TAG_START Still, Musk says Grok Bot has "no free tier," so Grok Bot usage is paid. That gives SpaceX a floor under its data centers that a 90-day exit clause can't take away, even if Grok's sales stay a fraction of what Anthropic pays. HTML_TAG_END

Huawei Expands Osta Lineup With a New 7-Inch Smartphone

Huawei’s Osta sub-brand could soon add another smartphone to its lineup. The company introduced the brand last week with the Osta O1 Pro, which is essentially a rebranded Pura X View. Now, a new leak has revealed details about the standard Osta O1.

According to the source on Weibo, the Osta O1 will feature a 7-inch display with a 1.5K resolution. The screen will reportedly have curves on all four sides. It is also expected to run on a Kirin 9020-series chipset.

The source describes the device as a slim smartphone with a large display. He also compares it to Huawei’s Mate Air series. The handset has reportedly completed its registration process and is now awaiting an official launch.

Interestingly, Huawei recently announced the Mate 90 Air with similar specifications. The phone features a 7-inch OLED display with a 1320 x 2760 resolution. It also runs on the Kirin 9020 chipset and measures just 6.6mm thick.

This raises the possibility that Huawei could reuse the Mate 90 Air’s hardware for the Osta O1. The main difference reported so far is its quad-curved display. An earlier leak also mentioned a large circular camera island on the back.

When a Weibo commenter questioned the need for another similar phone, the source said the brand was expanding. However, Huawei has not officially confirmed the Osta O1’s specifications or launch date.

Estonia closing land borders with Russia to keep out spies

Estonia is set to close its land border crossings to Russia by December 1. Families living on either side of the border are preparing for a long separation. Estonia’s government says that the closures are necessary to prevent Russian intelligence agents from carrying out attacks.

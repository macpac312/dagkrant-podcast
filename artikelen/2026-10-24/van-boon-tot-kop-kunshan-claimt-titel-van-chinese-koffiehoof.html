From bean to cup, Kunshan stakes claim as China’s coffee capital

SHANGHAI: Just over a year after the integrated development of the Yangtze River Delta region was elevated to a national strategy, US coffee giant Starbucks has chosen Kunshan, East China’s Jiangsu province, for its largest coffee manufacturing investment outside the United States – an early bet on the city’s ability to connect global supply chains with China’s vast consumer market.

The investment has since become part of broader efforts by the Kunshan government to make coffee a new industrial growth engine.

That ambition was on display at the 2026 Kunshan International Specialty Coffee Exhibition last week, which brought together more than 230 brands from 21 countries and regions.

Under a three-year action plan covering 2025 through 2027, Kunshan aims to build a 100-billion-yuan coffee industry and develop a broader coffee plus culture and consumer ecosystem.

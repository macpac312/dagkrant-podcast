Huawei May Give Its Next Flip Phone a Whole New Shape

Huawei is reportedly preparing a wider Nova Flip for 2027, with a 6.3-inch screen that would give the compact foldable a shorter, broader shape when opened. The rumored redesign fits a trend across the global smartphone industry. Book-style foldables such as Apple’s iPhone Duo and Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold 8 have embraced wider proportions, and regular phones such as Huawei’s Pura X View are following suit. Huawei’s next nova Flip could bring that same shift to its clamshell lineup.

A Wider Huawei Flip

A Weibo tipster claims the next nova Flip will pair a 6.3-inch folding display with a Kirin 9-series processor, a step up from the Kirin 8030 in the current model. The leak leaves the exact chip, screen aspect ratio, and cover display details unclear.

Huawei launched the nova Flip S in China in October 2025. It kept the original nova Flip’s basic design and introduced new colors, a lower starting price, and a different chipset. Its 6.94-inch inner display is larger diagonally than the rumored 6.3-inch panel, which could still offer more horizontal room depending on its proportions.

Huawei has already tried this shape with the Pura X, a foldable with a 6.39-inch inner display and a 16:10 aspect ratio. In July, tipster Digital Chat Station said Huawei was working on a compact wide foldable for 2027. That fueled speculation about a Pura X successor. The latest leak points to the nova Flip, and Huawei has yet to confirm which lineup will get the new device.

The Trend Goes Beyond Foldables

Huawei brought wider proportions to a regular phone with the Pura X View, which has a 6.39-inch screen with a 16:9.5 aspect ratio. Coolpad has also introduced the Cool X Wide, a non-folding Android phone with a 6.38-inch, 16:10 display. Xiaomi and Vivo are reportedly exploring wider regular phones of their own.

A wider screen gives videos, games, and apps more horizontal space without requiring a taller phone. For a clamshell like the Nova Flip, the appeal would be a wider display when open and a compact body that still folds in half.

Motorola Razr Flex official-looking renders surface in two color options - Gizmochina

Fresh high-quality images of Motorola’s upcoming Razr Flex have appeared online, offering the clearest view yet of the company’s first wide-style foldable. The renders arrive just as interest builds around book-style foldables with a tablet-like aspect ratio over the traditional tall clamshell shape.

SammyGuru shared what appear to be official-looking renders of the Razr Flex in two finishes: Pantone Dark Botanical Garden and Pantone Coriander. The green Dark Botanical Garden version features a woven, fabric-textured back with a matching frame, while the beige Coriander model has a leather-like finish paired with a champagne frame.

Both show a raised pill-shaped camera island on the rear housing a dual-camera setup and flash. The cover display uses a centered hole-punch for the selfie camera, while the inner display places its punch-hole in the top-right corner. Power and volume buttons sit on the right side.

These images align with earlier leaks and confirm a compact folding form factor designed as Motorola’s answer to the Galaxy Z Fold 8 and the iPhone Duo. Previous reports point to a 4.97″ cover display and a 6.92″ main inner screen, along with a 50 MP main camera, 50 MP ultrawide, and a 5,000 mAh battery.

The dimensions suggest the Razr Flex will be smaller than both the Z Fold 8 and iPhone Duo, which could also translate to a more accessible price. The hinge is said to allow the phone to stand on its own when half-open.

Motorola has not officially confirmed the device, but the consistency across recent image leaks and the level of detail in these renders indicate the design is largely finalized. The company is expected to reveal the Razr Flex next month or in early 2027, likely positioning it as a more compact and potentially more affordable alternative in the growing wide-foldable segment.

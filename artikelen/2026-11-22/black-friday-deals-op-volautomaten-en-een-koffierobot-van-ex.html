What the De’Longhi Magnifica Evo offers

The De’Longhi Magnifica Evo is a fully automatic espresso machine with 7 one-touch recipes, including espresso, cappuccino, latte, coffee, and plain hot water. Selections are made using simple buttons on the top face, and you can adjust brew strength (three levels), cup size (small espresso up to large travel mug), and temperature (three levels). You can also set a MyLatte personalized drink setting for day-to-day convenience.

For the grinding of beans, the Magnifica Evo supports 13 grind sizes. It uses a conical burr grinder for consistency, and you can even use pre-ground coffee with the front door flap that bypasses the grinder altogether. With a power output of 1,250 watts and a pump pressure of 15 bar, the machine handles the pulling and brewing process well.

The LatteCrema system is an automatic milk frother that preps frothed milk simultaneously with the coffee, combining them into the finished drink automatically. It supports dairy milk and plant-based milk alternatives, and the machine handles it all for you. The milk carafe detaches, allowing you to store any remaining milk in your fridge between brews.

Cleaning is simple. The milk frother and drip tray come off and can be cleaned in a dishwasher, and the machine itself has automatic descaling and cleaning functions. The package comes with some descaling solution, and De’Longhi’s support resources include a cleaning how-to video. Expect to clean it about once a week.

Why this deal is worth it

At $550, the De’Longhi Magnifica Evo is an excellent value. Automatic espresso machines rarely come this cheap, and when they do, you have to do all of the grinding, tamping, pulling, and frothing manually. With an Early Black Friday savings of $350, you can pay around the same price and automate most of the hard work—perfect for lazy or busy mornings.

On Amazon, it’s the #1 best seller in the Super-Automatic Espresso Machines category, and over 500 units were bought in the last month. User reviews are mostly positive with a decent rating over 1,000+ reviews, with users praising its ease of use, coffee strength, and compact footprint. Some users complain about small latte portions and noise while grinding.

The De’Longhi Magnifica Evo is ideal for anyone who wants to save money on lattes and cappuccinos without the fuss of actually making the drink by hand. It’s not ideal for anyone who mainly drinks black coffee or straight espresso, as you’ll be paying for a milk frother and advanced functions that go unused. And though it’s relatively compact, it still takes up a good amount of counter space—not great if your kitchen is tight.

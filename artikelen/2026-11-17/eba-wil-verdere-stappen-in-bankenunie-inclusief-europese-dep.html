EBA supports further progress on the Banking Union, including on deposit insurance | European Banking Authority

The EBA supports further progress on the Banking Union, including on deposit insurance, as a key step towards a more efficient and resilient European banking system.

Contrary to what may be understood from recent press coverage, the EBA is not working on alternatives to creating a common deposit guarantee scheme.

The European Commission has announced that it will present proposals on the deposit insurance framework as part of its wider banking package, expected in the first quarter of 2027. The EBA's role is to provide technical input and evidence to support the Commission and the co-legislators in this work.

As highlighted by the Commission in its July 2026 communication, the integration of the single market for banking can be fostered through a range of complementary initiatives.

The EBA is contributing to these efforts through work and proposals following up on its October 2025 report on efficiency.

François-Louis Michaud, EBA Chair, said: "Europe needs a more integrated banking market so that a diversified banking sector can keep adequately financing the economy. A truly single market will require tackling a number of current obstacles. Deposit insurance is an important part of that discussion. The EBA will continue to support the Commission and the co-legislators with evidence and technical advice."

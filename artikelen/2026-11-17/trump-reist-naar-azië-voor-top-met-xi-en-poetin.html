Today's top stories

Travelers are expected to face continued disruptions at airports as airlines recoverfrom Friday's nationwide ground stop. As of Sunday evening, more than 2,300 flights have been canceled, and more than 8,000 flights within, into or out of the U.S. have been delayed, according to FlightAware.com. Dallas-Fort Worth and Atlanta airports saw the most disruptions, with over 250 delayed flights each. U.S. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy said L3Harris Technologies Inc., a federal contractor, was doing routine maintenance in at least one remote location, changing copper wire to fiber optics.

President Trump is traveling to the Philippines and China this morning for a high-profile trip, which includes a trilateral summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin. This marks Trump's second visit to China this year. The trip to Asia comes as the administration faces scrutiny following Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's resignation. Huang admitted to an extramarital affair with a woman linked to China's top spy agency, according to The New York Times. He traveled to China with Trump earlier this year; this time, tech billionaire Elon Musk is accompanying Trump to Shenzhen.

Route 66 is celebrating an incredible century as America's most iconic highway. Stretching across eight states — from the vibrant streets of Chicago to the sun-soaked shores of Santa Monica, Calif. — this legendary route invites you to embrace the spirit of adventure on the open road. Imagine cruising through vast plains, rolling prairies, stunning canyons and shimmering deserts. Along the way, you'll discover a mix of big cities, charming cow towns and ghost towns. Over the decades, Route 66 has inspired countless songs, been captured in books, been seen in artwork and been celebrated in Pixar's 2006 animated movie Cars.

Williams, Aston Martin among new F1 liveries for Las Vegas GP as Acura &quot;replaces&quot; Honda

Williams and Aston Martin are among the F1 teams to have revamped their car liveries for this weekend's Las Vegas Grand&nbsp;Prix. F1 heads to Las Vegas for its third of three US races, with the entertainment capital having quickly become one of the series' key commercial weekends for teams and their partners. As a result, several teams are rolling out bespoke liveries this week, with Williams deploying a blue chrome livery on the FW48 races by Alex Albon and Carlos Sainz.

Aston Martin livery Photo by: Aston Martin

Meanwhile, Aston Martin is replaces all of its main logos of power unit partner Honda with those of Acura, Honda's luxury brand which is primarily active in North America. Acura will adorn the nose, engine cover and driver race suits of the AMR-26 as part of a long line of US takeovers for Honda's F1 teams. Fernando Alonso's and Lance Stroll's cars will also receive additional notes of white on the nose, sidepod and engine cover at the behest of sponsor CoreWeave.

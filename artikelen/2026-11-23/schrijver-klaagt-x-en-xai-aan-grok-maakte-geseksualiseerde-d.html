Grok created sexualized deepfakes of me, N.J. author claims in suit

A prominent New Jersey author and founder of MuslimGirl.com filed a lawsuit on Nov. 18 against Elon Musk's X and xAI alleging that the Grok artificial intelligence tool generated sexualized deepfake images of her, according to court documents.

The legal complaint filed in the Northern District of California claims the technology produced images that stripped activist Amani Al-Khatahtbeh of her clothing and her hijab.

The lawsuit targets both the X social media platform, formerly known as Twitter, and the xAI corporation for their alleged roles in creating and spreading the offensive images.

"I am taking Elon Musk to court," she said. "My legal team and I are officially filing a complaint against xAI for allowing Grok to be used as a weapon against our bodies and our identities."

Al-Khatahtbeh's attorney claims in court documents that on Jan. 2, 2026, Al-Khatahtbeh learned that X accounts directed Grok to create inappropriate images of her from an original picture that included her wearing a hijab and fur coat. The AI produced images were highly sexualized depicting her in bikinis and underwear without her hijab or her fur coat, the lawsuit claims.

Some of the images also included racial slurs, her attorney claims in court documents.

The suit also claims xAI had insufficient guardrails to prevent users from generating offensive content, like other AI companies do, and that xAI failed to adequately respond after being warned about their existence.

Al-Khatahtbeh is a well-known activist and New Jersey native who established MuslimGirl.com as a platform to amplify the voices of Muslim women. She graduated from Rutgers and is from East Brunswick.

"For a Muslim woman, having her image non-consensually manipulated to strip away her hijab and other clothing is a profound invasion of personal dignity, bodily autonomy, and religious freedom," the New Jersey chapter of the Council on American-Islamic Relations said in a statement supporting Al-Khatahtbeh's lawsuit. "Tech companies cannot hide behind innovation while allowing their platforms to be weaponized for misogynistic, sexist and anti-Muslim abuse."

Grok is an artificial intelligence chatbot developed by Musk's xAI company and integrated into the X social media service for user interaction.

"These images were not only meant to sexually humiliate Plaintiff, but to shame her for her religion, cultural customs, and identity," her attorney wrote in the complaint.

A representative from X and xAI did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

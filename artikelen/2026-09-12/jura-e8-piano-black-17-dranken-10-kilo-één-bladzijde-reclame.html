Jura E8 Piano Black Automatic Coffee Machine - 17 Drinks And Compact 22 lb Design

Jura E8 Piano Black Automatic Coffee Machine, model 15648, brings 17 programmable specialties and a compact 22 lb body into US kitchens. For home espresso fans, its 64 oz water tank and 1450 W power matter most.

The Jura E8 Piano Black Automatic Coffee Machine, sold in the US as the E8 Piano Black (NAC) with article number 15648, targets home coffee drinkers who want a full espresso bar in a compact footprint. With 17 programmable specialties, a 64 oz water tank and an integrated grinder, it turns beans into espresso, cappuccino and long coffee with minimal manual work.

Design, Dimensions And Daily Footprint

The E8 Piano Black (NAC) is classified by Jura as a mid range automatic espresso machine and is physically sized for a standard countertop. The machine is listed at 22 lbs in weight, with dimensions of 11 in width, 13.8 in height and 17.6 in depth on the US Jura specifications page for the Piano Black 15648 model.The same listing confirms the Piano Black (NAC) designation and made in Portugal origin. For US kitchens this means the machine needs a little under 18 in of counter depth and just under 14 in of clearance under cabinets.

The exterior of the E8 Piano Black combines a black housing with a 3.5 in TFT color display and chrome effect spouts. ShopJura describes the front interface as a 3.5 in color screen paired with physical buttons, designed to let users select from 17 specialties and adjust parameters without navigating deep menus.The same ShopJura listing specifies that the E8 Piano Black uses the HP3 milk system and includes a Fine Foam Frother G3, which affects how the machine handles milk based drinks.

Brewing System, Grinder And Drink Range

Under the housing, the Jura E8 Piano Black uses Jura's P.A.G.2 grinder and an advanced brewing system to prepare coffee specialties. The manufacturer comparison tools show that the E8 Piano Black NAD configuration uses a P.A.G.2 grinder, which is a multi level grinder designed to adjust grind settings before and after grinding for different drinks.In the comparison, Jura lists 17 specialties, 10 coffee strength levels and a brewing chamber capacity of 5-16 g, which applies across the current E8 platform.

On the ShopJura mid range listing for the E8 Piano Black NAC, Jura states that there is one grinder, a powder chute for pre ground coffee and 10 programmable coffee strength levels.The specification table on that page lists the brewing chamber capacity as 5-16 g and confirms the 17 individually programmed specialties. That capacity range defines the amount of ground coffee the machine uses per shot or drink type and the 10 strength steps indicate how finely users can adjust intensity.

For water, Jura's comparison data for the E8 Piano Black NAD shows a tank capacity of 64 oz / 1.9 l, voltage and power at 120 V and 1450 W, and a current draw of 10 A.These figures apply to the US market version and anchor the machine clearly in the household espresso segment. A 64 oz tank translates into several dozen espresso shots or multiple large milk drinks before a refill, and 1450 W is typical for a pump driven espresso machine that needs fast heat up and steam capacity.

Milk System, Cleaning And Connectivity

The Jura E8 Piano Black NAC uses the HP3 milk system and Fine Foam Frother G3 to prepare cappuccino and latte style drinks, relying on a hose that draws milk from an external container into the frothing unit. ShopJura describes the Fine Foam Frother G3 as using a redesigned internal structure to produce professional quality milk foam for both hot milk and foam focused drinks.The listing also highlights an optimized fluid system that offers one touch milk system cleaning and notes that the Intelligent Water System with RFID detects the CLEARYL Smart+ water filter automatically. The separate hot water spout supports making tea or Americanos without routing water through the coffee outlet.

Connectivity is structured around Jura's J.O.E. mobile app and a small Wi Fi Connect dongle. Both the ShopJura listing and Jura's own specifications emphasize that the E8 Piano Black is compatible with Wi Fi Connect but that the accessory is not included in the box.The ShopJura page states explicitly that Wi Fi Connect is not included with the purchase of the E8. This means buyers who want app control for drink selection and adjustment need to purchase the dongle separately.

Model Year, Manufacturing And US Entity

Jura maintains several regional variants of the E8 in different market codes, but the US Piano Black NAC configuration is documented as a 2023 model year machine made in Portugal. The US product page for the Piano Black NAC lists the article number and product code as 15648, Piano Black (NAC), with a model year of 2023 and country of production as Portugal.The same page lists a UPC code of 794151412477 and confirms the 120 V power specification, aligning the machine with US electrical standards.

For the newer NAD configuration, Jura's comparison page lists a model year of 2025, water tank capacity at 64 oz / 1.9 l and dimensions slightly narrower at 10.6 in width, 13.7 in height and 17.6 in depth, also at 22 lbs weight.The NAD variant product page confirms these dimensions and lists the article number as 15777, Piano Black (NAD), with UPC code 794151412972. Structurally, both NAC and NAD configurations sit in the same class, but the NAD is documented as the newer 2025 iteration.

The US sales entity behind the E8 line is Jura Inc., headquartered in Montvale, New Jersey. The specifications and comparison pages for the E8 list the contact address as JURA Inc., P.O. Box 9, 20 Craig Road, Montvale, NJ 07645, USA with a phone number of 1 800 220 5701.This positions Jura Inc. as the manufacturer and distributor of E8 machines in the US, even though the machines are physically produced in Portugal.

Comparison Within Jura's Lineup And Use Case

Within Jura's home portfolio, the E8 occupies the mid range segment, sitting above compact machines like the ENA line and below the flagship Z10 or Giga models in price and capability. The comparison tool lists the E8 with 17 specialties and a 64 oz water tank, while higher tier machines often offer more drinks, larger tanks or dual grinders.At the same time, entry level Jura models tend to offer fewer drinks and smaller tanks, which can change how often users refill and how diverse their menu can be. This positions the E8 Piano Black as a machine for households that want a broad drink range but do not need the size or complexity of Jura's top tier units.

The Gen 4 and Gen 5 references in US retail illustrate how Jura iterates within the E8 nameplate. ShopJura labels the E8 Piano Black NAC as Gen 4, with the same model number 15648 and the familiar dimensions and drink count.Crate & Barrel's description of a Gen 5 E8 in Piano Black at around $2,599.95 adds another generation marker. In practice, for a US buyer comparing options, the choice often comes down to how much value they place on incremental updates versus the concrete specifications like the 64 oz tank, 1450 W power and 17 drink programs.

Context And Verdict

Looked at in context, the Jura E8 Piano Black NAC is a mid range fully automatic espresso machine for US households that want a wide drink range and consistent automatic operation rather than manual espresso routines. With 17 specialties, a 64 oz tank and 10 strength levels, it aligns more with a built in counter bar than a simple drip maker.The combination of P.A.G.2 grinder, HP3 milk system and Fine Foam Frother G3 brings a layered set of capabilities, anchored by compact dimensions and a weight manageable for installation but not frequent moving.

Compared with simpler capsule machines or entry level pump espresso models, the E8 Piano Black commands a substantially higher price, around $2,799.00 in direct sales and around $2,599.95 to $2,799.95 at mainstream US retailers for later generations.This price level makes the machine more appropriate for committed coffee households than casual users, especially those who want milk drinks and have space for a dedicated machine. The compact footprint and integrated grinder justify the positioning for users who prioritize automation, but anyone focused purely on simple black coffee could find more basic options adequate.

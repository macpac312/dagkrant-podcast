Google Deepmind's Project Genie update brings Genie 4, chainable worlds, and a community gallery for remixing

Google Deepmind is releasing Genie 4, its most advanced world model for generating interactive 3D worlds. The new version, previously available only as a research preview, is part of a major update to Project Genie and is now open to Google AI Ultra subscribers. Genie 4 lets users reshape worlds through text input in real time, changing weather, characters, or storylines. The model also generates native real-time sound that adapts to the scene.

The older Genie 3 is getting updates too: longer sessions of up to three minutes, 360-degree panorama support, and chainable worlds. A new AI agent helps build worlds from spoken descriptions, and a community gallery lets users share and remix creations. All generated content carries SynthID watermarks and goes through safety checks. Google AI Pro members can join a waitlist.

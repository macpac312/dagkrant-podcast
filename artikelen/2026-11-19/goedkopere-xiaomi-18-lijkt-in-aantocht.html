A cheaper Xiaomi 18 phone is coming, it seems

In summary:

Whether Xiaomi is intentionally following in the footsteps of Apple or not, it has just launched Pro-level smartphones with a regular model missing.

2026 is a topsy-turvy year for tech and phones in particular and this is just one of them. Instead of launching a full line-up of phones in one go, Xiaomi unveiled the 18 Fold earlier in the year and yesterday gave the 18 Pro devices with a rear screen a global launch.

With costs spiralling, it’s likely leaving plenty of consumers wondering where the cheaper options are. Whether this is down to component prices and other constraints or not, they are coming.

For the regular Xiaomi 18 flagship (we loved the Xiaomi 17 and previous generations), it’s now been spotted, effectively confirming that it could be on the way soon.

The image comes from Gadgetsdata on X, although without details of where the photo was taken or who is holding the phone. The assumption is that it’s a Xiaomi executive – possibly papped at the firm’s launch event in Paris.

It looks similar to its predecessor and we can see the white back, metal frame and square camera module with three cameras. It actually looks quite a lot like the recently launched Oppo Find X10.

The big difference between the phone and the Xiaomi 18 Pro models, as expected, is the lack of a rear screen. The price will be key for many though, as they start at £1,199. With the Xiaomi 17 arriving at £899, this regular model will be more budget-friendly.

Gadgetsdata has more for us in the way of expected specs, with highlights including a 200Mp main sensor, large battery and a flat OLED screen. I’ve listed them below for ease.

When we’ll hear about the Xiaomi 18 officially is hard to say. It could be a quiet launch in the next few weeks or the firm might wait until the new year. Either way, we’ll keep you posted.

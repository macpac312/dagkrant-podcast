The **Xiaomi 18 Pro Max** has arrived on the global market with a 2.9-inch rear display, two 200MP **Leica**cameras and a 7,050mAh battery. However, the international version has a significantly smaller battery than its Chinese counterpart, according to a [review](https://rozetked.me/reviews/49510-obzor-xiaomi-18-pro-max-a-mozhno-esche-odin-ekran) by Russian tech blogger Rozetked.

The flagship runs on **Qualcomm’s Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6** processor and features a 6.9-inch AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate. It also introduces a privacy feature designed to prevent people nearby from reading the screen.

But the most unusual addition sits on the back of the phone, next to its cameras.

## What can the second screen actually do?

![Image 2](https://cdn-uz.kursiv.media/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/q87smwqn4lsxlcsljepjg-1024x576.jpg)

The rear AMOLED display measures 2.9 inches and supports a 120Hz refresh rate. Its main advantage is allowing users to take selfies and record videos using the more powerful rear cameras while seeing themselves on screen.

Content creators can also use a built-in teleprompter, while a dual-preview mode lets both the photographer and their subject check the framing.

Beyond photography, the smaller display shows notifications, incoming calls, timers and music controls. Users can customise it with widgets, animated backgrounds and even virtual pets that respond to gestures or weather conditions.

There are limitations, however. Although you can view notifications on the rear panel, you still need the main display to reply to messages.

The front screen has another unusual feature called the Privacy Display. It restricts viewing angles to make messages and other sensitive content harder to read from the side. Protection can be applied to individual apps or selected parts of the interface rather than the entire screen.

![Image 3](https://cdn-uz.kursiv.media/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/maxresdefault-4-1024x576.jpg)

Xiaomi has equipped the 18 Pro Max with a 200MP main camera, a 200MP periscope telephoto lens offering 3.2x optical zoom and a 50MP ultra-wide camera. The front-facing camera also has a 50 MP sensor.

The main camera uses LOFIC HDR technology to preserve detail in scenes with extreme differences between light and shadow, such as illuminated buildings at night.

During testing, Rozetked particularly praised the telephoto camera for portraits and close-ups. It can focus on objects from just 12cm away, making it suitable for macro photography.

Performance is another selling point. The Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 delivers fast app loading and smooth everyday operation, although the reviewers found that the phone noticeably reduced its performance under prolonged heavy loads.

The global version runs **[HyperOS 4](https://uz.kursiv.media/en/2026-11-05/xiaomi-hyperos-4/)**based on **Android 17**, with **Google**services, eSIM and **Android Auto** available from the start.

## Why does the global version have a smaller battery?

The Chinese Xiaomi 18 Pro Max comes with an 8,500mAh battery, while the international version drops to 7,050mAh, a reduction of around 17%.

Despite the difference, Rozetked recorded approximately seven to eight hours of active screen time during daily use, including photography, messaging and navigation.

The phone supports 100W wired and 50W wireless charging.

Its size may also be a drawback. At approximately 240g, the device is noticeably heavy, while its large camera module makes it top-heavy and somewhat awkward to operate with one hand.

Huawei shows off wide smartphone from brand-new series just ahead of launch

After a series of leaks, it's finally official: The Huawei OSTA O1 Pro will be unveiled on Friday, November 20. It is the first smartphone from Huawei's new OSTA brand. Huawei is already showing the smartphone in white, purple and black.

Apart from the color gradient on the purple version and the OSTA branding, the design is almost identical to that of the Huawei Pura X View. That's largely because the OSTA O1 Pro appears to use the same 6.39-inch OLED display, with a 15:9 aspect ratio instead of the roughly 20:9 ratio more common on smartphones. At around 14.4 cm tall, the device is almost a centimeter shorter than the Google Pixel 11, but at 8.7 cm wide it's also about one and a half centimeters wider.

Huawei lists variants with 12 GB to 16 GB of RAM and 256 GB to 1 TB of flash storage. The top-end model's memory and storage specifications suggest that the OSTA O1 Pro is a flagship smartphone. That lines up with rumors that the device features a HiSilicon Kirin 9030S, just like the Pura X View.

If Huawei carries over the rest of the Pura X View's hardware as well, the OSTA O1 Pro would feature a 200 MP main camera, a 50 MP telephoto camera, an 8 MP ultrawide camera, and a relatively large 7,000 mAh battery. Huawei is expected to confirm details about the specifications, pricing, and availability by Friday's launch at the latest.

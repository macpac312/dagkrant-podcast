## How much oil is going through Hormuz? Why data doesn’t match US claims

The United States and Iran continue to make competing claims about who has greater control of the critical Strait of Hormuz in the Gulf.

Washington insists the strait remains open, with dozens of vessels transporting millions of barrels of crude daily.

Last month, US President Donald Trump claimed “total control” over the corridor, a route that normally carries a fifth of global petroleum and gas supplies in peacetime.

Yet the passage has stayed shut since the joint US-Israel war on Iran erupted six months ago.

Tehran maintains it commands the waterway, keeping it shut to all but pre-cleared ships navigating prescribed lanes. Any unauthorised traffic, it cautions, remains liable to attack.

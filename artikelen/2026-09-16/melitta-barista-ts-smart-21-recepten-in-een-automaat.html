Melitta Barista TS Smart F86/0-100 - 21 Coffee Recipes

Melitta Barista TS Smart F86/0-100 offers 21 coffee recipes and app control. Pricing lands at £649.99 as of 09/15/2026.

Melitta Barista TS Smart F86/0-100 puts 21 customizable coffee recipes in a stainless-steel bean-to-cup machine that can be controlled through the Connect App. The UK product page lists a launch price of £649.99 and shows the machine in stock.

Control And Capacity

The machine is described as a fully automatic coffee machine with app control and a dual-chamber bean hopper for different beans. Melitta's UK page also lists 1.8 L capacity and 1450 W power, both concrete figures that define the machine's footprint in the countertop coffee segment.

The same page says the model can save coffee favorites for up to eight people. That makes the user memory more than a novelty, since it ties recipes to named profiles rather than a single default setup.

North American Melitta Base

Melitta North America lists its headquarters in Clearwater, Florida, and says it serves customers across the US and Canada. The company also says it has a coffee manufacturing, packaging, and innovation center in Cherry Hill, New Jersey.

Where It Fits

For buyers comparing it with simpler bean-to-cup machines, the eight-profile memory and two-bean hopper are the standout numbers in the spec sheet. The model is aimed at households that want automatic espresso-style drinks without moving up to a commercial unit.

Compared with the category standard of basic countertop brewers, the 21 recipes and 1.8 L tank put it in a more feature-rich lane. That combination matters most for kitchens that want more drink choice than a single-serve system.

Bottom Line

Melitta Barista TS Smart F86/0-100 is a feature-heavy bean-to-cup machine with app control, 21 recipes, and a 1.8 L water tank. It suits users who want programmable coffee drinks and profile memory, while the countertop format keeps it in the home appliance lane.

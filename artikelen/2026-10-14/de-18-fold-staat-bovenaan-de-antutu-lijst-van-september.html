Xiaomi 18 Fold leads AnTuTu's September flagship rankings, Xiaomi Pad 9 Pro Max tops tablets

AnTuTu published its September performance charts based on average AnTuTu 12 scores from devices tested in China. Xiaomi took first place in the flagship and tablet categories, while Oppo won the mid-range segment with the Reno15 Pro.

AnTuTu released its monthly device rankings for September, and Xiaomi claimed the top spot in two of the three categories it tracks. The benchmark vendor compiles the charts from average AnTuTu 12 scores submitted by real devices, which means the numbers reflect sustained real-world performance rather than the peak score a phone posts in a single ideal run. All data comes from tests conducted in China, so the rankings track devices available in that market.

Flagships: the Xiaomi 18 Fold pulls ahead

The Xiaomi 18 Fold led the flagship category with an average score of 4,620,886. The foldable runs on Xiaomi's in-house XRing O3 chipset, and that result puts it ahead of every phone using Qualcomm's current flagship silicon.

That matters because the two runners-up both ship the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6. The iQOO 16 and the Honor Magic9 Pro Max sat in second and third place respectively, which gives the XRing O3 a measurable lead over Qualcomm's latest part in daily use.

Xiaomi placed three phones in the top nine. That concentration shows how much of the company's 2026 lineup leans on the XRing O3 and its own silicon tuning, a shift away from the Snapdragon dependence that defined its earlier flagships. You can read more about the Xiaomi 18 Fold on Xiaomi's site.

Mid-range: Oppo's Reno line takes the category

Oppo won the mid-range chart with the Reno15 Pro, which averaged 2,306,256. The iQOO Z11 followed, then the Oppo Reno15 and Oppo Reno16.

Oppo placed four devices in this list, including the top spot and two of the top four. The Reno series has carried Oppo's mid-range volume for years, and the Reno16's presence alongside the Reno15 suggests the newer model hasn't displaced its predecessor in buyer hands yet. Xiaomi responded with the Redmi Turbo 5 and the 17T, both sitting in the upper half of the chart.

Tablets: the Pad 9 Pro Max posts the highest score of any category

The Xiaomi Pad 9 Pro Max led the tablet rankings with an average of 5,233,432, the highest number across all three AnTuTu categories this month. Tablets benefit from larger chassis that dissipate heat better, which lets the same class of chipset sustain higher clocks for longer.

Two entries for the Lenovo Legion Y700 appear in the chart, which points to both generations still seeing active use. The iQOO Pad Ultra in second place runs the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 and ships with a built-in cooling fan, a design aimed squarely at gamers who want sustained performance during long sessions. RedMagic's Gaming Tablet 5 Pro in third takes the same approach.

What the charts reveal about the Chinese Android market

A few patterns hold across all three categories. BBK Electronics brands, which include iQOO, Oppo, Realme and OnePlus, filled most of the remaining slots after Xiaomi. Vivo appears separately from iQOO and Oppo, its corporate siblings, and each brand competes on its own footing.

Xiaomi's two wins also underline how the company now splits its bets. The 18 Fold and the Pad 9 Pro Max both sit at the top of their charts while running flagship-grade silicon, and the mid-range results show the company's cheaper Redmi and numbered lines holding position against Oppo's Reno volume. If you want context on how these chips compare overall, AnTuTu maintains a running chipset ranking on its site.

The scores come from devices sold in China, so availability elsewhere varies. The Xiaomi 18 Fold and the Chinese variants of several phones on these lists have no confirmed international release dates. AnTuTu plans to publish its October chart once the month's test data is compiled.

Source: AnTuTu, September 2026 rankings (in Chinese).

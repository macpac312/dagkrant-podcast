Motorola Razr Flex 2027 leaks point to a new Snapdragon chip, Android 17, and a wide design

Motorola spent years making foldable phones that could shrink to fit your pocket before finally going big with the Razr Fold. But having explored both ends of the foldable spectrum, the company’s next move could land somewhere in between. Earlier this month, we spotted the Motorola Razr Flex 2027 on the company’s own website, lending credibility to earlier leaks suggesting that Motorola was working on a different kind of foldable. Now, newly surfaced certification documents and an alleged image of the phone give us a clearer idea of what Motorola might have in mind, inside and out.

A security certification document identifies a Motorola device codenamed Parker (XT2761) running Android 17. That model number has already been linked to the Razr Flex 27 through regulatory filings in the UAE, but what’s particularly interesting is the processor listed inside: Qualcomm’s Snapdragon SM8845 Pro.

For those keen on this kind of stuff, you’ll know this isn’t the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 (SM8845) that earlier leaks had suggested. Instead, the document points to an unannounced chipset rumored to debut as the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 Pro. According to Notebookcheck, the upcoming OnePlus Turbo 7 Pro has also been linked to this processor.

Beyond the new processor, the document also points to Bluetooth 6.0, face unlock, seamless software updates, and an under-display fingerprint scanner, with the latter contradicting earlier leaks suggesting a side-mounted sensor. We also know from previous FCC filings that the Razr Flex 2027 will support Wi-Fi 7, ultra-wideband (UWB), 5G, NFC, and wireless charging.

A separate image shared on Reddit purportedly shows the Razr Flex 2027 from the back. The short, wide body, horizontal camera housing, and beige finish broadly match the concept renders we covered in September, although the camera module has a noticeably different finish. The image reportedly originates from Chinese sources, but we haven’t been able to independently verify its authenticity.

The proportions match earlier leaks suggesting a 6.92-inch, 165Hz folding display, a 4.97-inch cover screen, dual 50MP rear cameras, and a 5,000mAh battery with 25W wireless charging.

With Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold 8, Apple’s iPhone Duo, and Huawei’s Pura X family already exploring wider foldable designs, Motorola seems to want a slice of that market. However, the relatively small rumored displays suggest the company might be taking the compact approach a little further than its biggest rivals.

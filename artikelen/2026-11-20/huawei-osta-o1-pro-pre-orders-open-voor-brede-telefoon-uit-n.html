Huawei OSTA O1 Pro Roundup: Everything you need to know - Gizmochina

Huawei‘s new sub-brand OSTA, known in China as Xingmang, or Starlight, is the company’s first new smartphone line since Honor was spun off. Earlier reports described it as an online, cost-effective brand aimed at bringing Kirin chips and HarmonyOS to more buyers. Official materials now position its first product as a HarmonyOS AI phone.

Initial reports suggested the initial lineup would include two models, the OSTA O1 and the OSTA O1 Pro. But only the Pro variant has been confirmed, and it is already up for pre-order ahead of its Nov. 20 launch. Continue reading to know everything about it.

Huawei OSTA O1 Pro design, colors, and configurations

The official Vmall listing shows a wide-screen bar phone with a horizontal dual-camera module and an OSTA logo on the back. It comes in Starry Black, Nebula White, and Starburst Purple. Starry Black and Nebula White are offered in 12GB+256GB and 12GB+512GB, while Starburst Purple is reserved for the 16GB+512GB and 16GB+1TB configurations, leaving the 16GB memory options exclusive to the purple model.

Real-life photos that surfaced before the listing showed a plain back finish, rather than the textured rear on Huawei’s earlier wide-screen phone. Tipster Digital Chat Station puts the OSTA O1 Pro at 6.68mm thick and 200 grams, making it a slim, relatively light wide phone.

Huawei OSTA O1 Pro display and chip

Huawei has not published a full specification sheet yet, but Digital Chat Station has outlined a 6.39-inch 1.5K LTPS flat display with a 16:9.5 aspect ratio and 1.05mm bezels on all four sides, plus a Kirin 9030S chip. Those figures match the size, ratio, and bezel width of the Huawei Pura X View, whose official panel also has a 120Hz refresh rate and 6,500-nit peak brightness. Those extra display details have not been confirmed for the O1 Pro.

Huawei OSTA O1 Pro cameras, battery, and software

On cameras, the same tipster lists a 200-megapixel 1/1.28-inch RYYB main sensor, a 50-megapixel 3.7x RYYB periscope telephoto, and a Red Maple lens. Official renders show only two rear cameras, which lines up with reports that the O1 Pro drops the ultra-wide camera found on the Pura X View.

He also lists a 7,000mAh battery with 66W wired charging and wireless charging, plus a side fingerprint sensor. The wireless charging wattage has not been confirmed. Official materials describe the O1 Pro as a HarmonyOS AI phone, but Huawei has not detailed the software version or AI features yet. Those answers are expected at the November 20 launch.

Huawei OSTA O1 Pro price and availability

Pre-orders are open on Huawei’s official online store, Vmall, and the company has set the brand and phone launch for Nov. 20 at 2:30PM China time. The product page still shows no price and no sale start time.

The earlier online, cost-effective pitch suggests the O1 Pro could come in below the Pura X View, which starts at 5,999 Yuan (~$900) for the 12GB+256GB model. Until Huawei names a price, that remains only a reasonable expectation, not a confirmed figure.

How the OSTA O1 Pro differs from the Huawei Pura X View

The OSTA O1 Pro sits very close to the Pura X View. They share the Kirin 9030S, the 6.39-inch 16:9.5 panel with 1.05mm bezels, the 200-megapixel main camera and 50-megapixel 3.7x periscope, the Red Maple sensor, the 7,000mAh battery, 66W wired charging, a side fingerprint sensor, and a 6.68mm-thick body.

The Pura X View adds an 8-megapixel ultra-wide camera and a 50-megapixel front camera, plus official 50W wireless charging, IP68 and IP69 protection, and BeiDou satellite messaging. The O1 Pro appears to drop the ultra-wide, adds 16GB memory options the Pura X View does not offer, and is about a gram lighter if the leaked 200-gram figure holds. It also uses different colors, branding, and a plainer back. With the Pura X View already past 750,000 sales in China, this looks like a closely related wide-screen design rather than a confirmed rebadge, possibly aimed at a lower price.

What about the Huawei OSTA O1?

Early reports claimed the OSTA O1 could be based on the existing Huawei Mate 70 Air. That phone measured 6.6mm, used a 7-inch OLED screen, packed a 6,500mAh battery, and started at 4,199 yuan. At present, there is no clarity on whether the O1 will launch alongside the O1 Pro, and it has not appeared on Vmall.

Huawei is also holding a launch event on November 24 to unveil the Mate X8 foldable, alongside the Mate 90 Air, billed as the thinnest Mate bar phone yet, and the Watch Air. It will be interesting to see how the OSTA O1, said to be based on the Mate 70 Air, is positioned when the Mate 90 Air is set to debut.

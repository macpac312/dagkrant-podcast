Five drivers set for grid penalties for engine changes at F1 Las Vegas GP

Five drivers will take grid penalties for the Las Vegas Grand&nbsp;Prix, including new Formula 1 world champion Kimi Antonelli. As previously indicated by Mercedes,&nbsp;Antonelli will take a five-place grid drop for an internal combustion engine change – but does not pick up a further penalty for a turbocharger change due to still being within his allocation. Mercedes announced the 2026 F1 world champion would take on its upgraded turbo specification as permitted under&nbsp;ADUO, while Russell would stick with the current specification due to needing to serve a greater grid penalty for exceeding his allocation. McLaren’s Oscar Piastri will serve a 10-place grid penalty for an engine change – as previously indicated by the team – while Carlos Sainz will also serve a 10-place penalty after Williams opted to give him his fifth turbocharger of the season.

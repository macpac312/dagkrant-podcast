Kushner: Trump open to NATO-like security guarantees for Ukraine

US President Donald Trump is open to offering security guarantees to Ukraine ‘based on NATO’s Article 5’ mutual defence clause, special envoy Jared Kushner has said. Kushner also claimed Russia is ‘comfortable’ with the proposal.

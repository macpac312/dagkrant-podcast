Grok Bot Usage Limit Reached: Reset Time And Options (2026)

When Grok Bot stops replying and says you have hit your usage limit, xAI’s documentation says it is not broken: your weekly usage is used up.

The limit resets each week, but xAI’s pages do not give a day or hour.

This guide uses xAI’s own documentation, read on November 20, 2026, and keeps news reports clearly separate.

Quick answer: what the message means

Who has Grok Bot

xAI says Grok Bot is included with the Premium, Super and Ultra levels of The X Subscription.

Grok Bot draws from the same weekly usage pool as Cursor and the Grok apps, and Super and Ultra get larger pools than Premium.

Plans and included usage

The pricing page excludes sales tax and gives the included usage only as multiples, with no weekly amount in numbers.

What to do when the limit is reached

Keep extra usage under control

For The X Subscription, xAI says to leave Auto Top-up off or set a monthly limit under Additional usage credits at grok.com/dashboard/billing.

For legacy Cursor plans, turn off on-demand or set the On-demand monthly limit under Settings, then Usage and Billing.

A cap does not stop a Bot mid-run, so a running task can exceed it slightly.

What the usage banner offers

The settings page says Review Usage asks your Bot to review your usage for you when a warning appears.

At your weekly limit, the banner above the message box can offer Use Reset, which xAI says brings weekly usage back to 0% right away once you have waited.

Banners can also offer Invite a Friend, which opens xAI’s referral dialog.

If you are on a trial

xAI says some accounts can start a trial, which is a usage credit with a 7-day window and does not convert to a paid plan.

Usage is measured by agent steps and tokens, not message count, and used credit is not restored.

If you use Grok Bot through a legacy Cursor account

xAI says legacy Grok Bot usage is metered on your Cursor account, not on Grok or X.

Check it under Settings, then Usage and Billing, in Grok Bot.

On a legacy Cursor account you can link an individual SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus, SuperGrok Heavy or X Premium+ account, and Cursor uses whichever source grants more weekly usage; the sources do not add together.

If you upgrade SuperGrok after linking, xAI says to allow up to 24 hours for the limit to update.

Team Bots and the owner limit

If a team Bot owner reaches their limit, members may see “This Bot’s owner has reached their usage limit.”

The reply is billed to the owner, so the owner needs to check their plan.

What xAI does not say

What changed in November: reports

TeslaNorth reported on November 14, 2026 that Elon Musk said on November 1 the cost of serving Grok Bot was unsustainably high relative to revenue.

It also reported that on November 8 he replied to a user whose $200 plan ran out in 14 hours, and promised to investigate.

TeslaNorth says Grok Bot team member Sam Sokolin said on November 13 that optimizations gave about 7% more plan usage, and up to 15% on routine runs.

xAI’s help pages do not mention these changes, so treat them as reported by TeslaNorth, not confirmed by xAI.

Grok apps versus Grok Bot

xAI’s FAQ for the Grok website and apps says hitting the weekly limit there is not a hard stop and free-tier Chat and Voice continue.

That page says Extra Usage Credits start at $5 and can currently only be bought on the web.

It describes a different product from Grok Bot, so check which page applies to you; our SuperGrok Lite vs SuperGrok guide covers the SuperGrok side.

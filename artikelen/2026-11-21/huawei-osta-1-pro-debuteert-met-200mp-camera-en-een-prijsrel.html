Huawei Osta 1 Pro debuts

Following several reports from earlier this month, Huawei went ahead and announced its new Osta sub-brand in China. As revealed by Huawei exec Richard Yu, Osta stands for Original Smart Technology Accessible.

The Osta 1 Pro is the first device in its ranks and it’s a wide-screen phone featuring a 6.39-inch OLED with a 16:9.5 ratio, a 200MP main camera and a decently sized 7,000mAh battery.

If those specs sound a bit familiar, it’s because the Osta 1 Pro is essentially a rebadged Huawei Pura X View but without an ultrawide lens and no satellite messaging capabilities. The Osta 1 Pro is configurable with up to 16GB RAM, while the Pura X View maxes out at 12GB.

Outside of these minor spec differences, the Osta 1 Pro is virtually identical to the Pura X View. This means you still get Huawei’s Kirin 9030S chipset at the helm while the software front is covered by HarmonyOS 7. Osta 1 Pro’s UI features a liquid-glass look, and Huawei is also bringing a new Little Star AI assistant.

The back houses a 50MP periscope telephoto with 3.7x optical zoom alongside the 200MP main shooter. The phone features IP68/IP69 ingress protection, a side-mounted fingerprint scanner and a 50MP selfie cam. Huawei did not announce plans to bring its Osta devices outside of China for now.

Osta 1 Pro is available in Starlight Purple, Starry Black, and Nebula White colors with a starting price of CNY 5,499 ($824) for the 12/256GB trim. The top-tier 16GB RAM and 1TB storage version is priced at CNY 8,799 ($1,320).

SpaceXSI Denies Liability in UK Lawmaker’s Grok Lawsuit

SpaceX Super Intelligence has denied liability in Labour MP Jess Asato’s lawsuit at London’s High Court over sexualized images of her generated by its Grok AI. The company argues that images it left online were visibly fictional and therefore did not violate her privacy, and says it did not control the prompts users submitted; it says it removed content that breached its rules, including a video depicting a sexual assault scenario. Asato argues that Grok’s design and the company’s role in modifying prompts and publishing results make it responsible, and is seeking permanent technical safeguards to prevent nonconsensual manipulated images. The case is an early test of whether AI developers can be held legally accountable for harmful content produced by their systems.

Colorado Republicans Sue to Block Graduated Tax

Advance Colorado and two Republican state senators sued to block Colorado’s voter-approved Amendment 87, arguing it violated TABOR by combining a constitutional change with new graduated income-tax rates and that voters received inaccurate information about the measure’s costs. The amendment, which passed with about 51.7% of the vote, is projected to raise billions for education, health care and child care, while cutting taxes for most Coloradans.

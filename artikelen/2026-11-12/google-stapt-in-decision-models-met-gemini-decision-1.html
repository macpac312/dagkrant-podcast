Google joins the decision-model wave with Gemini Decision 1, scoring 1,000 questions in a single pass

Google Deepmind is introducing the Gemini Decisions API, an interface built for fast, closed-ended decisions like yes/no questions, multiple choice, and scale-based ratings. The underlying model, Gemini Decision 1, processes up to 1,000 independent questions in a single pass across text, images, audio, video, and PDFs. It scores options directly and returns probabilities with confidence values in 150 to 250 milliseconds. Google says it already uses the API internally for dynamic model routing in Gemini Notebook, design prototyping in Stitch, and safety checks in Google Search.

The API costs $0.10 per million input tokens with no output-token charge, about five times cheaper than Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite according to Google. It's available now through Google AI Studio, with integrations for OpenRouter, LangChain, Vercel, and other platforms rolling out.

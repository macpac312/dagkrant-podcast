Grok 4.20 Wins Live Trading Contest

The contest and the result

Alpha Arena Season 1.5 puts AI models head to head by trading real stocks with the same initial bankroll. Musk-led xAI's Grok 4.20 came out on top, beating submissions from OpenAI and Google, Alphabet Inc. (NASDAQ:GOOG) (NASDAQ:GOOGL).

An X Freeze post revealed the previously unnamed leaderboard entry as Grok 4.20 and reported it closed with about $11,060 in equity after starting at $10,000, which works out to roughly a 10% to 12% return. A screenshot shared with the post showed Grok variants sitting in four of the first six places.

How Grok was set up

The showing was achieved across several configurations of the same model - Situational Awareness, New Baseline, Max Leverage and Monk Mode - indicating the gains did not rely on a single approach. X Freeze added, "Grok 4.20 isn't just doing well on benchmarks," and, "It's making real money in live markets."

Why it caught attention

Elon Musk amplified the post and commented, "Ok, I think I see a way to pay for all those GPUs." The line taps into a key question for investors spending on AI infrastructure: can advanced models generate direct revenue streams, not just benchmark bragging rights?

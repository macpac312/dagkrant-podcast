Anthropic pushes ahead with pre-Thanksgiving IPO despite tech selloff, accepts lower valuation

Anthropic is pressing ahead with its IPO despite the tech selloff, accepting a lower valuation. The Claude developer wants to go public before Thanksgiving and raise $80–100 billion at a $1.25–1.75 trillion valuation, according to investors cited by the Financial Times. That tops the current $965 billion but falls short of the $2 trillion the most optimistic investors had hoped for. Annualized reported revenue now sits at about $81 billion, up from roughly $65 billion at the end of July.

Rising borrowing costs and heavy AI spending plans triggered a tech selloff this week. Democrats winning the US midterms accelerated it because they had promised to slow data center construction, including other measures. The Nasdaq 100 dropped 6.4 percent on Thursday but recovered somewhat on Friday.

Huawei&#8217;s rumored sub-brand may launch a high-priced flagship

Huawei’s planned smartphone sub-brand is expected to introduce a flagship-grade handset at a high price, according to Weibo leaker DigitalChatStation.

The leaker said the phone, referred to in previous reports as Starburst or Starlight, would have specifications comparable with top-tier devices. Huawei has not announced the sub-brand, its name or any handset under it.

A user responding to the leak speculated that the phone could cost about 8,000 yuan. DigitalChatStation did not provide a price, launch date or detailed specifications in the latest post.

Earlier reports described the first phone as a lower-priced alternative to Huawei’s Pura X View, aimed at younger buyers. The new information suggests the lineup could include models at different price points, including a flagship device.

The first handset is expected to use a wide-screen form factor similar to the Pura X View. It may feature a 6.39-inch LTPS display with a 16:9.5 aspect ratio and 1.05 mm bezels.

The device may be offered in black, white and purple. Reported memory and storage configurations include 12 GB with 256 GB, 12 GB with 512 GB, 16 GB with 512 GB, and 16 GB with 1 TB.

Other previously reported specifications include a Kirin 9020 chipset, a battery exceeding 7,000 mAh with 100W charging, advanced cooling and a rear camera system with a 50-megapixel wide-angle camera and a second-generation Red Maple color lens. The model may be called O1 Pro.

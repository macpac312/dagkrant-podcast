Elon Musk says Grok Bot is expensive to run, calls costs ‘unsustainable’ as users face usage limits- Moneycontrol.com

Elon Musk has acknowledged that operating Grok Bot is becoming financially challenging for xAI, with the cost of running the AI assistant exceeding the revenue it generates. The comments come amid reports from X users who say they are exhausting their Grok usage quotas faster than expected.

The discussion started when an X user, DogecoinNorway, asked Musk whether the platform could reset Grok Bot's usage limits. The user said several people had noticed their available usage running out quickly during the week.

Responding to the request, Musk pointed to the financial burden of operating the AI service. He said the cost of running Grok Bot was too high compared with the revenue it currently brings in, indicating that the company needs to address the economics of providing access to the chatbot.

The response did not include a commitment to reset usage limits or provide additional access to subscribers.

Grok Bot relies on computational resources to process user requests, generate responses and perform tasks. Operating these systems requires substantial infrastructure, including computing hardware, data centres and electricity.

The expenses can increase as more people use the service and as AI models handle increasingly complex requests. These factors make it difficult for companies to provide unrestricted access while keeping operating costs under control.

According to the report, Grok Bot has an estimated 800,000 paying users. The growing number of subscribers and the computational requirements of the service could be contributing to the financial pressure on xAI.

Several X Premium users have recently reported that their weekly usage allowances are being exhausted sooner than expected. However, the company has not provided a detailed explanation of the reported changes or confirmed whether the limits have been adjusted across subscription tiers.

Musk's comments suggest that xAI is facing pressure to balance the cost of operating Grok Bot with the revenue generated through subscriptions.

The company could explore changes to its pricing structure, usage allowances or infrastructure to address the issue. However, xAI has not announced any specific changes to subscription plans or confirmed when existing limits might be revised.

For now, users who rely on Grok Bot for frequent queries may continue to encounter usage restrictions. The extent to which these limits will change remains unclear as xAI works towards making the service financially sustainable.

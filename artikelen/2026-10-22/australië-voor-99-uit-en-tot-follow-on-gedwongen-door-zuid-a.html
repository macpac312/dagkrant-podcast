Australia bowled out for 99 and trail by 170 runs at 156-4 having been forced to follow on by South Africa in men’s second Test – as it happened

Stumps: Australia 156-4 (Head 87, Boland 2), trail by 170

For the second evening in a row, Boland survives as nightwatcher, seeing out a Harmer maiden as six cacthers hover around him.

He has Head for company at the close, who will sleep tonight with 87 unbeaten runs to his name. He was streaky at times, and was put down on five, but he remains. With him, so do Australia’s hopes of an unlikely draw.

South Africa picked up nine Australian first innings wickets today with Jansen claiming a five-for before enforcing the follow on.

The early wicket of Maddinson for Rabada vindicated that call but Head and Labuschagne dug in until the latter lost his head to Harmer and was caught in the deep.

That led to a small collapse as Renshaw was bowled shouldering arms to Rabada and Webster was pinned lbw by Harmer.

With Smith nursing a neck injury, this is a daunting challenge for the Australians.

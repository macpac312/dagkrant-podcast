Xiaomi 18 Pro and Pro Max: specifications and release date

Xiaomi will hold a global launch event for its flagship smartphones, the Xiaomi 18 Pro and Xiaomi 18 Pro Max, on 17 November 2026 in Paris. The new models feature additional displays on the rear panel, powerful next-generation Snapdragon processors and dual 200-megapixel Leica cameras.

Specifications of the Xiaomi 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max

After launch the Xiaomi 18 Fold The Chinese manufacturer is expanding its international range of flagship smartphones. Both models in the Xiaomi 18 Pro series have been on sale in China since 23 September.

The compact Xiaomi 18 Pro features a 6.4-inch OLED display and a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 processor. The top-of-the-range model features a 6.9-inch screen and the more powerful Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6.

Both devices feature a 200-megapixel Leica main and telephoto camera, and run on HyperOS 4, based on Android 17.

One of the key features of smartphones is the additional screen integrated into the camera module on the back. It allows you to view notifications, control music, set timers and use the main camera for selfies.

Global versions may be fitted with smaller batteries

Chinese version Xiaomi 18 Pro It is fitted with a 7,000 mAh battery, whilst the Pro Max has a battery capacity of 8,500 mAh.

However, according to preliminary data, the international versions will be fitted with 6,000 and 7,050 mAh batteries respectively. The wired charging power will be 90 W for the entry-level model and 100 W for the higher-end model.

How much will the Xiaomi 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max cost?

In China, the Xiaomi 18 Pro starts at 5,999 yuan (approximately $900), whilst the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max starts at 6,999 yuan (around $1,050).

Xiaomi will announce the official European prices, memory configurations and launch dates on 17 November. The date on which the smartphones will go on sale in Ukraine is not yet known.

Qwen4-Max is built on a **new architecture for the Qwen4 series**, with **2.4T backbone parameters, 71B activated per token, and an additional 328B N-gram Embedding parameters**. The architecture systematically upgrades the sparse MoE model across **attention, residual connections, and embeddings**, improving computational efficiency, model capacity, and training stability:

* **GDN + Qwen Sparse Attention (QSA):** Three Gated DeltaNet layers compress history into fixed-size states for every QSA layer. QSA uses a lightweight indexer to select relevant context at micro-block granularity, reducing both attention and indexing costs for long sequences. * **Gated Residual (GR):** Four parallel residual branches use dynamic read and write gates to strengthen information flow across layers and improve training stability. * **N-gram Embedding:** Context-based lookups provide representations of local token patterns at the second layer, expanding model capacity with little additional computation.

![Image 3: Qwen4-Max architecture with GDN and QSA layers, four Gated Residual branches, and N-gram Embedding.](https://qianwen-res.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/Qwen4-Max/architecture.png#center)

Training at the 2.4T-parameter scale progressed smoothly, with loss decreasing steadily and **no loss spikes throughout the run**. We attribute this stability primarily to improvements in model architecture and training optimization.

#### Blender: 3D Modeling and Scene Creation

Qwen4-Max demonstrates strong 3D creation capabilities. It uses Blender to carry out geometry modeling, scene assembly, material setup, lighting, and rendering, progressively turning creative ideas into detailed 3D works with a distinct visual style. Across architecture, interiors, natural landscapes, and science-fiction scenes, it demonstrates an integrated understanding of spatial layout, object structure, and visual appearance.

These 3D assets are in practical used in games and brought into physical objects through 3D printing. This illustrates the model’s potential to connect digital creation with downstream production workflows, supporting design exploration, content creation, and prototyping.

#### GameDev: Create Games with Real Engines

Qwen4-Max works fluently with leading game engines, including Unity, Unreal Engine, and Godot. From a single natural-language brief, it handles scene construction, gameplay logic, integration of suitable external assets, material setup, HUD design, and visual effects to deliver a playable game. During development, it runs the game and uses visual and interaction feedback to refine camera tracking, character animation, hit feedback, lighting, and pacing. Through repeated testing and iteration, it brings scenes, gameplay, and sound together into a cohesive 3D game experience.

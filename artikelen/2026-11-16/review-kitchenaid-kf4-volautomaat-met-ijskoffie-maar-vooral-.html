KitchenAid KF4 5KES8454 Fully Automatic Espresso Machine with Iced Coffee Review

Iced coffee might be the headline feature of the KitchenAid KF4 Fully Automatic Espresso Machine but that’s selling this multi-tasking bean-to-cup a bit short. Looked at as a space-saving coffee maker than can do almost everything its bigger siblings can, iced coffee is more the cherry on top of an already pretty good cake. It offers everything you’d hope for: named profiles with personalised drinks, auto ground coffee recognition, and it’ll even froth non-dairy milks. It could use more guidance for iced drinks in general though. Plus, the interface isn’t as smooth as you’d hope, sometimes requiring several taps to get to where you want to go. However, these are small wrinkles in what is otherwise a fairly smooth brewing process. Now the only real reason to visit your local café on scorching hot days may be the air con.

* Brews coffee at a lower temperature * Space-saving design * Foams milk and non-dairy milks

* No guide to how much ice to add * Shallow cup platform * Interface can be frustrating

**Once a seasonal treat, iced coffee has become a year-round first choice for many. It can be notoriously difficult to get right though.**

Overextraction, for example, can affect flavour, which when combined with ice, makes for a cold but bland drink. KitchenAid’s KF4 Fully Automatic Espresso Machine seeks to make iced coffee foolproof. Two dedicated settings – Iced Coffee and Iced Espresso – brew at a lower temperature to preserve taste, while all the other programmes offer a choice of temperatures from low to high. And, just like some of its bigger brethren, it’ll froth plant milks as well as dairy. All you need to do is bring the ice.

* Can save favourites to profiles * Easy milk system cleaning * 14 standard drinks including two iced

Bean-to-cup coffee machines can be bulky. While it’s a stretch to call the KF4 compact next to a simple espresso machine, it’s far slimmer than many other bean-to-cup machines. However, that doesn’t apply to all dimensions. It’s noticeably deep on the average worktop, and a little taller than some, such as the [KitchenAid KF8](https://www.trustedreviews.com/reviews/kitchenaid-kf8-fully-automatic-espresso-machine), too.

![Image 7: KitchenAid KF4 5KES8454 Fully Automatic Espresso Machine with Iced Coffee on the counter](https://www.trustedreviews.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/7/2026/07/Fully-Automatic-Espresso-Machine-with-Iced-Coffee-KF4-3-820x461.jpeg)

The KF4 could reasonably tuck beneath shelves but you’ll need to be able to access the 1.8L water tank, and the bean hopper and ground coffee chute on top.

![Image 8: KitchenAid KF4 5KES8454 Fully Automatic Espresso Machine with Iced Coffee bean hopper](https://www.trustedreviews.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/7/2026/07/Fully-Automatic-Espresso-Machine-with-Iced-Coffee-KF4-2-820x461.jpeg)

And while it might be 3kg lighter than the KF8, there are trade-offs. Colour choice is limited to Matte Black or Porcelain White, and the exterior is mostly plastic.

Despite that, there’s still plenty of attention to detail: filling the tank can be done in-situ thanks to a hinged flip-up lid, or the whole carried to the sink using an integral handle. The KF4 uses the same connected milk-frothing system as the KF8 that foams or warms easily and is cleaned automatically after use. And while the KF4 has a shallower cup platform, it’s still wide enough for big mugs.

![Image 9: KitchenAid KF4 5KES8454 Fully Automatic Espresso Machine with Iced Coffee drip tray](https://www.trustedreviews.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/7/2026/07/Fully-Automatic-Espresso-Machine-with-Iced-Coffee-KF4-1-820x461.jpeg)

There’s a smaller 8.9cm / 3.5-inch touchscreen than the KF8 but it’s as simple to use.

![Image 10: KitchenAid KF4 5KES8454 Fully Automatic Espresso Machine with Iced Coffee touchscreen](https://www.trustedreviews.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/7/2026/07/Fully-Automatic-Espresso-Machine-with-Iced-Coffee-KF4-12-820x461.jpeg)

The menu offers 14 drinks, two of which are iced options, with the ability for coffees to toggle one or two shots, select bean strength, body, temperature and volume. Favourite combinations can be saved to one of four named profiles and resaved over should your preferences change.

![Image 11: KitchenAid KF4 5KES8454 Fully Automatic Espresso Machine with Iced Coffee milk drink option](https://www.trustedreviews.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/7/2026/07/Fully-Automatic-Espresso-Machine-with-Iced-Coffee-KF4-7-820x461.jpeg)

Plus, there are reminders to clean the milk hose, descale and deep clean, all with guided steps. None of the removable parts, such as the drip tray or milk container lid are dishwasher safe, though, so there’s still a small amount of maintenance by hand.

* Makes easy milk-based drinks * Brews coffee with consistent crema * Could use more guidance for iced drinks

Huawei OSTA O1 Pro appears in leaked photos ahead of launch | Межа

A leak from China has shown the wide-screen OSTA O1 Pro ahead of its planned November 20 introduction.

The OSTA O1 Pro is the wide-screen model in Huawei’s upcoming lineup. Its size and design resemble the Pura X View, with a similar wide body but one fewer camera.

Huawei’s Pura X View had sold around 449,800 units through late September. The O1 Pro keeps much of its look but has two rear camera sensors instead of the Pura X View’s more elaborate arrangement. It also replaces the textured back panel with a plain finish.

The OSTA O1 Pro reportedly has a 6.39-inch 1.5K LTPS flat display with an expected 16:9.5 aspect ratio. All four bezels are said to measure around 1.05mm. The phone is expected to use Huawei’s Kirin 9030 processor and come in four memory and storage configurations.

Buyers will reportedly be able to choose from Starry Black, Nebula White and Starburst Purple. Huawei has not officially confirmed the OSTA O1 Pro’s design, but is expected to do so ahead of launch. The OSTA O1 and OSTA O1 Pro are due to be introduced on November 20.

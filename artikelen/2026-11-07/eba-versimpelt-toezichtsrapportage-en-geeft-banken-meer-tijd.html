The EBA outlines next steps on supervisory reporting simplification and extends implementation timelines following consultation feedback on the ITS | European Banking Authority

The European Banking Authority (EBA) today outlines the way forward for the finalisation of proposals to simplify EU supervisory reporting, following extensive dialogue with the banking industry and feedback received during the public consultation. The package that is currently being finalised will introduce additional simplification measures, greater proportionality and extended implementation timelines, reducing reporting costs while preserving the information necessary for effective supervision. The final report on the draft Implementing Technical Standards (ITS) on supervisory reporting will be published by the EBA in December 2026.

Building on consultation feedback, industry workshops and discussions with the Reporting Contact Group (RCG) of the Joint Bank Reporting Committee (JBRC), the EBA has further refined its proposals to address the main concerns raised by stakeholders and deliver a tangible reduction in reporting requirements.

The EBA and national competent authorities are also taking steps to reduce reporting burden beyond the EBA framework.

The EBA Board of Supervisors’ members have committed to reducing overlapping national and supervisory data requests where equivalent information is collected through the EBA reporting framework, acknowledging that supervisors may need access to other data for the performance of their tasks. This will promote greater consistency, reduce duplication and encourage the use of common definitions and concepts across the EU.

The EBA will publish the final report on the draft ITS package in December 2026 and will submit it to the EU Commission for adoptions. Institutions will benefit from at least a one-year implementation period before the application of the new requirements, with longer implementation periods for selected reporting areas.

To support implementation, in early 2027 the EBA will publish the accompanying technical package, including the data point model, taxonomies and validation rules.

During the transition period, institutions and competent authorities should (1) continue to follow the EBA Opinion on IFRS-18 reporting; (2) to take into account the EBA consideration on the application of the FRTB from 1 January 2027; (3) to report the existing reporting templates for operational risk losses where the data to be reported should include only the data not affected by the new granular operational risk taxonomy; and (4) for supervisory benchmarking reporting for credit risk and IFRS 9 benchmarking the institutions covered by the benchmarking exercise should continue using 2026 ITS on supervisory benchmarking reporting.

On 10 April 2026 the EBA published proposals for the revisions of the European Commission’s Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/3117 (ITS on Supervisory reporting) for the public consultation that ended on 10 July. In parallel, the EBA also consulted on the proposals for the revision of ITS on supervisory benchmarking reporting with a view of integrating the reporting requirements into the ITS on supervisory reporting.

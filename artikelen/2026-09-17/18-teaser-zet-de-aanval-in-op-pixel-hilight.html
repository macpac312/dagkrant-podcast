Xiaomi 18 launch teaser takes aim at Pixel HiLight

Following its launch of the 18 Fold, to get it in just before the iPhone Duo, Xiaomi has now teased the launch event for the rest of its 2026 flagship range.

The so-called ‘Techtember’ rolls on and plenty more high-end phones are on the way before we reach ‘Techtober’. Xiaomi wants your attention for its next event, which is likely to take place in the coming days.

The official Xiaomi 18 series launch event teaser has landed on Weibo, and I can’t help feel like it takes a stab at Google. The Pixel 11 Pro range added a HiLight feature which sits in the camera flash, but it can only tell you who is calling or turn blue while Gemini is thinking.

Meanwhile, Xiaomi has confirmed its new 18 Pro model will feature a “brand-new, versatile back screen” (translated). While Xiaomi has been offering rear screens for a while, the short video with its colours dancing around the edge and “Generating” in Chinese feels very much ‘look what we can do’.

The way those lights are running around the edge of the rear display is exactly like how I imagined Google might implement the feature on the Pixel 11 (see below). So, perhaps it can outclass HiLight (previously known as Pixel Glow) without even factoring in what the screen can show elsewhere.

We can also see three rear cameras like the Xiaomi 17 Pro.

The question on our lips is where this phone will launch. While Western markets like the UK (but not the US) do get flagship Xiaomi phones, we’re typically offered the regular flagship model as well as the Ultra version.

However, like Oppo is doing, Xiaomi is reportedly ditching the Ultra from the line-up. While that’s a shame for top camera phone fans, it might mean we get one or both of the Pro and Pro Max models instead.

We’ll have to wait for the exact launch date, but that could be announced today, so we’ll update this article as and when.

Rumoured specs include 100W charging and to be the first phones with Qualcomm’s new flagship Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 Pro chipset, which is set to be announced next week on 22 September, so Xiaomi may also choose this date.

The EBA publishes updated list of validation rules as part of its regular quarterly reporting frameworks update | European Banking Authority

The European Banking Authority (EBA) issued today an updated list of validation rules defined in its reporting frameworks, as part of its regular quarterly review process. The revised package identifies rules that (i) have been deactivated due to inaccuracies or IT-related issues, or (ii) have been reactivated.

Competent authorities across the EU are reminded that data submitted in accordance with the Implementing Technical Standards (ITS) and Guidelines should not be formally validated against deactivated rules.

With the introduction of DPM 2.0 from release 4.0 onwards, validation rules are now embedded directly in both the taxonomy and DPM. This integration improves consistency in implementation by reporting institutions, enhances traceability of changes, and contributes to a more efficient and harmonised supervisory reporting process.

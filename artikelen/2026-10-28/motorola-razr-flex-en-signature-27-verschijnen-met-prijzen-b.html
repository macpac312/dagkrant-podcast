Motorola Signature 27 and Razr Flex show up at a European retailer with prices

Motorola showed off the Signature 27 at Qualcomm's Snapdragon event last month, but it hasn't revealed the full specs or pricing yet. Now, Portuguese retailer MegaPC has listed the Motorola Signature 27, and reportedly the upcoming Motorola Razr Flex as well.

The Signature 27 is listed in the Pantone Coal Smoke color with 12GB of RAM and 512GB of storage for €1,594.90. The page is currently marked as temporarily out of stock, and there's no word on when it will ship.

The description mostly repeats what Motorola has already said: the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 chipset, a 200MP periscope telephoto camera, a 50MP Sony Lytia 910 main camera, and audio tuned by Bang & Olufsen. It also mentions an aerospace-grade aluminum frame, ceramic glass, and a 3D vapor chamber for cooling. For reference, this is the successor to the Motorola Signature that launched earlier this year.

The Razr Flex is the more interesting part. It appeared in two separate listings, one for the Pantone Coriander color and one for Pantone Dark Botanical Garden, both with 12GB of RAM and 256GB of storage for €1,510.90. Those colors match what we've heard about the wide foldable before, which has also shown up on Geekbench recently with a Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset and Android 17. That leak also pointed to a mid-December launch.

That said, we'd take the Razr Flex listings with a grain of salt. The pages only showed a placeholder image, and the generic description even called it a "clamshell", which is what the Razr 70 family is, but not a wide book-style foldable. A price that's only €84 below the Signature 27 also looks odd, so it may well be a placeholder. Retailer prices can obviously change before sales begin anyway.

“My recollection of that Richards innings at Durban in 1970 is that Ali Bacher ran himself out in the last over before lunch trying to get Barry on strike so he could go for it,” writes Nick Robb. “It was Barry’s second Test, not his first which was what Mark Nicholas said on commentary. With Bacher gone, Richards and Pollock proceeded to put on 102 in the first hour after lunch, Barry then departing for 140. Ian Chappell said to Stackpole during their partnership ‘Now we’re in real trouble – whatever Richards makes, that bastard will double.’ He was wrong: Graeme fell short after making 274.”

iPhone Duo vs Xiaomi 18 Fold: Which is the better foldable flagship in 2027?

Released in late 2026, the iPhone Duo and Xiaomi 18 Fold deliver flagship performance in foldable form. Both companies use in-house chipsets, similarly sized displays, impressive imaging hardware, and more. So, choosing the right phone can be tricky for potential customers looking to upgrade in late 2027.

Hence, this article compares the key specifications of the iPhone Duo and the Xiaomi 18 Fold to help readers make an informed purchasing decision.

iPhone Duo vs Xiaomi 18 Fold: A detailed comparison

Build material and display

Apple uses a grade 5 Titanium metal frame for rigidity and durability. The iPhone Duo has a glass back panel along with a glass display. The glass back panel comes with Ceramic Shield protection, and the display is protected by Ceramic Shield 2.

On the other hand, Xiaomi uses a 7000-series aluminum alloy midframe and a glass back panel. Xiaomi protects both the back glass panel and the outer display with Xiaomi Dragon Crystal Glass 3.0. The company has also incorporated a double-layer ultra-thin glass structure on the foldable inner screen.

The iPhone Duo features a 5.4-inch outer and a 7.6-inch inner display. It also features a Super Retina XDR display for enhanced eye protection during long hours of use. The iPhone screen also supports the P3 color gamut for color-accurate visuals, and the 120 Hz refresh rate keeps scrolling smooth and reduces motion blur for an impressive gaming experience.

Xiaomi included a 5.38-inch outer screen and a 7.58-inch inner screen. It is slightly smaller than the iPhone Duo's display. Xiaomi also uses LTPO AMOLED panels for sharp, color-accurate visuals, plus an adaptive refresh rate that helps save battery life. The standard 120 Hz refresh rate keeps scrolling smooth and reduces motion blur for a better gaming experience.

Chipset and performance

Apple's A20 Pro has already secured its spot among the best SoCs released in 2026. Built on an advanced 2nm process, Apple's in-house chipset can reach a max boost speed of 4.90 GHz. It also includes a 7-core iGPU with Neural Accelerators, which can easily meet pro gamers' demands.

Meanwhile, Xiaomi has also incorporated the in-house XRING O3 chipset in the Xiaomi 18 Fold. Built on a 3nm process, this deca-core chipset can boost to a max speed of 4.35 GHz. It uses the ARM Mali G2-Ultra iGPU to handle graphics and gaming.

Camera and battery

Apple equipped the iPhone Duo with the standard 48MP dual-camera setup found on regular iPhones. Apple also added a 12MP front camera. However, with a 200MP main camera, a 50MP periscope telephoto, and a 50MP ultrawide camera, the Xiaomi 18 Fold offers much better imaging hardware. Xiaomi also includes dual 16MP front cameras.

As for the battery, Apple included a 4883 mAh battery in the foldable flagship, while the Xiaomi 18 Fold packs a 6000 mAh battery. So, the Xiaomi phone can easily outlast the iPhone Duo on a single charge.

Final verdict: Which is the better mobile?

Overall, the slightly bigger screen and superior SoC currently make a strong case for Apple's foldable flagship. However, customers who prioritize imaging hardware and battery capacity can also consider the Xiaomi 18 Fold. The iPhone runs on iOS, while the Xiaomi phone delivers a foldable flagship experience on Android. This could also be a determining factor for some.

Note that since the Xiaomi 18 Fold is currently sold only in Mainland China, we can't compare the prices of these two smartphones.

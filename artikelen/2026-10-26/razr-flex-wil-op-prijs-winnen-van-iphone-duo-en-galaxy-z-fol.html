Motorola is preparing to launch its new foldable for the year, the Moto Razr Flex, which will compete with the recently launched Apple iPhone Duo and Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8. It features a compact, passport-style design and the top-of-the-line 2025 Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 SoC. While it uses a previous-year chipset, Motorola may use this to undercut the Apple iPhone Duo’s flagship price in price-sensitive markets, especially in India, where the Duo starts at Rs 299900.

Key Highlights

It may also undercut Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold 8, which starts at Rs 174999. So if you are looking to upgrade to a new compact foldable this year, besides Apple and Samsung, you can also consider Motorola’s Razr Flex.

Motorola hasn’t announced an official release date for the Razr Flex yet, but a recent Geekbench 6 listing and rumors suggest it’s gearing up for a global launch soon.

Before launch, here are some leaked specs and features you should know.

Motorola Razr Flex Spotted Online – Specifications and Features

Motorola Razr Flex was recently spotted on Geekbench 6, hinting that the Moto Razr Flex will pack the power of the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 5, the 2025 processor built on a 3nm process.

A new listing on Geekbench 6 suggests the new Motorola Razr Flex will come with up to 12GB of RAM and hints that the new passport-style Motorola foldable will be called the Moto Razr Flex 27.

Other leaked specs suggest the next Motorola foldable, the Razr Flex, will feature a 4.97-inch OLED panel on the outside with Dolby Vision and HDR10+ support.

Inside, there will be a foldable 6.92-inch OLED panel with the same Dolby Vision and HDR10+ support.

Camera and Battery

On the camera end, speculation suggests that the Motorola Razr Flex will have a dual rear camera with a 50MP main and a 50MP ultrawide sensor, plus a 32MP selfie shooter.

On the battery and charging side, the Moto Razr Flex will pack a larger 5000mAh dual-cell battery, with each panel having a 2500mAh cell. Leaks also suggest it will support 24W Qi2 wireless charging.

Software and Connectivity Features

The next Moto Razr Flex foldable phone will support Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6. Reports also suggest the Razr Flex may get IP48 and IP49 water- and dust-resistance ratings.

On the software front, we will get a new Moto OS called HelloUI, which is based on Android 17 with new AI features.

Motorola Razr Flex to Take on Apple iPhone Duo and Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 – How?

Apple iPhone Duo started the trend of compact, passport-style foldables, and later saw thesee the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold, which introduced an Android alternative to the iPhone Duo.

However, if you want a cheaper yet premium compact foldable alternative, the Motorola Razr Flex fills that gap.

While the Apple iPhone Duo is selling for Rs 299900 and the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 for Rs 174999, we speculate the Motorola Razr Flex will be priced below Rs 1,60,000 while keeping last year’s Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 processor.

What is the Motorola Razr Flex?

The Motorola Razr Flex is an upcoming passport-style foldable smartphone set to compete with premium foldables like the Apple iPhone Duo and Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8. Leaks suggest it will pack a Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset and a dual-OLED display.

What processor will the Moto Razr Flex use?

The Moto Razr Flex is tipped to feature the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 processor built on a 3nm process. A Geekbench 6 listing points to this chipset and up to 12GB of RAM.

What are the expected display specifications of the Moto Razr Flex?

The Moto Razr Flex is rumoured to have a 4.97-inch external OLED display and a 6.92-inch foldable OLED inner display. Both panels are expected to support Dolby Vision and HDR10+.

When will the Motorola Razr Flex launch?

Motorola has not officially announced a launch date for the Razr Flex. A Geekbench 6 listing and leaks suggest the device may be preparing for launch, but the global release timeline remains unconfirmed.

How does the Moto Razr Flex compare with the Apple iPhone Duo and Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8?

The Moto Razr Flex could offer a more affordable alternative to Apple's and Samsung's premium foldables. Leaked specifications include a Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset, two OLED displays, a 5,000mAh battery, and 50MP dual rear cameras. Its performance and value will depend on the final specifications and launch price.

(04:45 GMT) ## Maternity services to resume at Gaza’s Al-Aqsa Hospital

The Palestinian Ministry of Health says maternity services at Al-Aqsa Hospital in Deir el-Balah will resume today after repairs to damage caused by Israeli attacks were completed.

In a statement, the ministry said the maternity services were temporarily transferred to Al-Awda Hospital in Nuseirat during the Israeli “aggression and genocide” as part of “exceptional measures” taken to redistribute health services and enhance the emergency response.

It thanked the staff at Al-Awda Hospital as well as the Palestine Children’s Relief Foundation for its support in rehabilitating the maternity department at Al Aqsa Hospital.

## Houthi siege of Taiz threatening lives of patients, Yemeni rights network says

The Yemeni Network for Rights and Freedoms (YNRF) has condemned Houthi attacks on and siege of Taiz, saying the actions “threaten patients’ lives and deepen the humanitarian catastrophe facing the population”.

According to the organisation, as a result of the attacks and the siege:

* Six kidney dialysis patients have died and approximately 1,280 patients with kidney failure face the risk of treatment interruption. * Two medical personnel have been killed, and four others wounded. * Two hospitals, four medical centres and three medical warehouses have been damaged, with an additional 42 healthcare facilities facing acute supply shortages. * Twenty childbirth and antenatal care clinics have been closed, depriving 734 childbirth cases of services and medical supervision.

It added, “Fuel and medical supply shortages also threaten the lives of 192 intensive care patients and 154 infants in incubators, making the continuity of medical services a matter of life and death.”

## Saudi-led coalition claims attacks on Houthi tunnels, missile launchers

The Saudi-led coalition says it has carried out a military operation “aimed at neutralising sources of threat” following Houthi attacks on infrastructure in Saudi Arabia and Yemen.

In a statement on X, the coalition said it destroyed five tunnels used by the Houthis to store ballistic missiles and four platforms used to launch ballistic missiles. It also claimed an attack on a missile depot and said it “focused on cutting off Houthi militia supply lines between areas under their control”.

The operation also targeted Houthi “fortifications, gatherings of their members, and their military equipment along the front lines inside Yemen”, it added. * 47m ago

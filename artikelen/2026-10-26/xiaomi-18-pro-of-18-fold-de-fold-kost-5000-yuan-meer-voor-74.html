Xiaomi 18 Pro vs Xiaomi 18 Fold: Which Flagship to Buy in 2026? - Memeburn

For most buyers, the Xiaomi 18 Pro is the better phone for the money, because it starts at ¥5,999 against ¥10,999 for the Xiaomi 18 Fold and still carries the larger main camera sensor, the 200MP telephoto, the bigger battery, faster charging and a stronger dust rating. The Fold earns its premium only through its 7.58-inch inner display, which by Memeburn’s calculation offers about 74% more screen area than the Pro, and through six-app multitasking, so it suits people who work on their phone rather than people who mainly shoot and scroll.

Prices checked October 19, 2026. Pro weight and thickness come from a third-party spec listing, as Xiaomi’s launch post did not state them.

What Does the Extra ¥5,000 Actually Buy?

The Xiaomi 18 Fold’s inner display is 18.4% larger than the Xiaomi 18 Pro’s 6.4-inch screen by diagonal, but because it uses a near-square aspect ratio of about 1.41:1, close to standard paper, according toIT之家, the gain in area is far larger. Memeburn’s calculation puts the inner panel at about 27.1 square inches against 15.6 for the Pro’s tall 2436 x 1120 screen, a 74% increase, which works out to roughly ¥433 for every extra square inch at the entry price.

The Fold’s 5.38-inch outer screen, by the same arithmetic, offers about 13.5 square inches, around 13% less than the Pro, so the phone is a smaller handset when closed and a much larger one when open. The premium also narrows as storage rises, falling from 83.3% at 256GB to 62.5% at 16GB and 512GB, since the gap stays at ¥5,000 across the first three tiers before reaching ¥6,000 at 1TB.

Which Xiaomi Has the Better Cameras on Paper?

The Xiaomi 18 Fold’s main camera uses the same Samsung HP5 sensor, at 1/1.56 inches, that the Xiaomi 18 Pro uses for its 75mm telephoto, according toAnTuTu’s spec breakdownandIT之家’s Pro launch report. The Pro’s own main camera sits on a 1/1.28-inch Light Hunter 960, which by nominal optical format offers roughly 50% more sensor area, and that gap usually shows in low light and natural background blur.

The telephoto difference is wider still, because the Fold’s 50MP JN5 is a 1/2.75-inch sensor behind an f/2.8 lens, while the Pro’s 200MP periscope has roughly three times the sensor area by the same nominal measure and focuses from 12cm for close-ups. The Fold reaches slightly further at 80mm against 75mm, but the Pro should hold more detail at that range on paper, and both phones let users frame selfies with the rear cameras, the Pro through its rear screen and the Fold through its outer display.

Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 or XRING O3: Does the Chip Change Anything?

The Xiaomi 18 Pro uses Qualcomm’s 2nm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 with CPU cores up to 5GHz, while the Xiaomi 18 Fold runs Xiaomi’s own 3nm XRING O3 with a 10-core CPU up to 4.35GHz, a 16-core GPU and a 200 TOPS NPU. Xiaomi claims 60% faster multi-core CPU and 85% faster graphics over its previous XRING chip, but Memeburn has not found a like-for-like independent benchmark or sustained gaming test of the Fold, so any performance verdict between the two would be a guess.

What is clear is where the Fold’s hardware goes, since Xiaomi pairs the XRING O3 with up to six apps on screen at once and compatibility with desktop-style tools such as WPS and the Wind financial terminal. Whether that holds up depends on how well individual apps resize, which is the weak point every large-screen Android phone still shares.

Does the Xiaomi 18 Pro Last Longer on a Charge?

The Xiaomi 18 Pro’s 7,000mAh battery is 16.7% larger than the Xiaomi 18 Fold’s typical 6,000mAh, or about 19% larger against the Fold’s rated 5,875mAh, and it charges at 100W against 67W. The Fold also has to power a 7.58-inch inner panel at up to 4,000 nits, so the Pro’s lead in real use may be wider than the capacity gap suggests, although no matched battery test of the two has been published.

Is the Xiaomi 18 Fold Harder to Live With Day to Day?

The Xiaomi 18 Fold weighs 219g, about 16g more than the roughly 203g listed for the Xiaomi 18 Pro byNanoReview, and when closed it measures 117.8mm tall, 83.6mm wide and 10.68mm thick, which makes it 34mm shorter but 11.6mm wider and 2.2mm thicker than the Pro. The bigger practical gap is dust protection, since the Fold’s IP58 and IP59 ratings mean it is dust-protected rather than dust-tight, while the Pro carries IP66, IP68 and IP69.

Availability may decide the matter outside China, because Xiaomi has said the 18 Pro series is going global this year, as Memeburn covered in itsglobal launch report, while no international release has been announced for the Fold, according toMacRumors. Which Xiaomi Flagship Should You Buy?

How much more does the Xiaomi 18 Fold cost than the Xiaomi 18 Pro?

The Xiaomi 18 Fold starts at ¥10,999 against ¥5,999 for the Xiaomi 18 Pro, a ¥5,000 or 83.3% premium at 12GB and 256GB that narrows to 62.5% at 16GB and 512GB.

Does the Xiaomi 18 Fold have a better camera than the Xiaomi 18 Pro?

The Xiaomi 18 Fold does not have the better camera hardware on paper, because its main sensor is smaller at 1/1.56 inches and its 50MP telephoto is far smaller than the Xiaomi 18 Pro’s 200MP periscope.

Is the Xiaomi 18 Fold available outside China?

Xiaomi has not announced an international release for the 18 Fold, while it has said the Xiaomi 18 Pro series will launch globally in 2026.

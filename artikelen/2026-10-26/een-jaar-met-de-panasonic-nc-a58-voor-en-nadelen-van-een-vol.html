I have been using the Panasonic NC-A58 fully automatic coffee maker for about a year now, ever since I reviewed it on YouTube last summer. It has now become an essential part of my work and relaxation time. In this article, I have summarized the pros and cons of using it, so I hope this will be helpful for those who are considering purchasing one.

What kind of coffee maker is it?

It is a Panasonic coffee maker that handles everything from grinding beans to brewing and cleaning the mill automatically. You can choose from three brewing courses—Strong, Rich, and Mild—and two grinding methods—coarse and medium-fine—for a total of six combinations. You can change the flavor to suit your mood that day.

Easily enjoy freshly ground coffee at home

With just the push of a button, you can enjoy delicious, freshly ground coffee. The taste and aroma are authentic and incomparable to instant coffee. The wonderful aroma of coffee spreads through the room while it is brewing, which alone is enough to lift my spirits. Being able to drink a cafe-quality cup while at home has definitely improved my quality of life.

Enjoy a variety of flavors

As I mentioned at the beginning, you can enjoy six different flavors by combining the three courses (Strong, Rich, Mild) with the two types of filters (coarse and medium-fine). My personal favorite is "coarse grind x strong," but since I can change the flavor depending on my mood, I don't get tired of the same beans. There is also a decaf bean course, which I find very useful for late-night work sessions🌙

Almost no maintenance required for the mill

I had the impression that coffee makers with mills were difficult to clean, but this product automatically cleans the mill section every time it is used, so I have rarely had to spend time cleaning the mill even after a year of use. While periodic maintenance like citric acid cleaning is necessary, not having to disassemble and clean the mill every time was easier than I imagined.

It is loud

The sound when grinding beans is loud and lasts for about 20 seconds. Depending on your living environment, you might want to be mindful of the time of day you use it. Since I live in a rental apartment, I try to avoid using it early in the morning or late at night.

Coffee bean fragments fly out

When in use, some ground beans scatter around the main unit and the area where the pot is placed. Because of this, I make sure to wipe it down quickly whenever I notice it.

If there is water left in the bean container, the beans tend to clog

This is more of a precaution than a con, but I once had the beans clog because I used it while the bean container wasn't completely dry. The manual advises wiping it with a cloth after every use, so it was entirely my own carelessness. At my home, we leave the container lid open to dry after use, and we haven't had any bean clogs since.

Although there are a few things that bother me, overall, I am very satisfied with this machine. Even after a year, it has become an essential item for my work and relaxation time. ☕️ Want to drink delicious coffee easily ☕️ Want to enjoy a variety of flavors as those are the people I recommend it to.

Doogee Note60 W looks like the Huawei Pura X View, is wide like the Pura X View, but is not the Pura X View

Doogee has presented the Note60 W series at the 140th Canton Fair in Guangzhou, according to a post on the company's official WeChat account. The phone is on display in Hall 20.2 at booths G59 to G64. The phone has a 6.38-inch wide display with a 90 Hz refresh rate. Doogee has not confirmed the panel type, aspect ratio or resolution, though an LCD is expected. The phone is 8.4 mm thick, has a 6,500 mAh battery and ships with Android 17.

Doogee's launch poster follows Huawei's official Pura X View promotional image almost shot for shot. Both show a red phone held in two hands, back on the left and front on the right, with a horizontal racetrack-shaped camera module, three lenses and a flash, a striped rear texture, a vertical logo in the lower-left corner and a red orb wallpaper. The differences are visible on closer inspection. The Note60 W has a noticeably thicker bottom bezel, while the Pura X View has uniform bezels on all four sides. The Pura X View's lock screen also shows the stacked HarmonyOS clock, which is absent from Doogee's render.

Doogee has not announced the chipset, memory, cameras, price or availability of the Note60 W, though the phone should be available in the coming month.

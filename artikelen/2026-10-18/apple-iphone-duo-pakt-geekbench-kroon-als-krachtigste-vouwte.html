Apple's iPhone Duo Claims Geekbench Crown as Most Powerful Foldable — BigGo Finance

Apple's first foldable has posted benchmark numbers that put it ahead of every other folding phone on the market, just two days before pre-orders open.

The iPhone Duo appeared on Geekbench 7 with the model identifier "iPhone 19,4," packing the A20 Pro chip and 12 GB of RAM. It delivered a single-core score of 3,986 and a multi-core score of 11,067. Those figures are not the highest ever recorded for an A20 Pro device -- some iPhone 18 Pro runs on the platform have edged higher -- but they land near the median for the chipset.

That consistency matters. It suggests the foldable will behave much like its candybar siblings in everyday use, without the thermal or performance compromises that have historically plagued folding devices. It also means the iPhone Duo enters the segment as the most powerful foldable smartphone yet benchmarked, surpassing the current leader, the Xiaomi 18 Fold, which relies on Xiaomi's in-house Xring O3 silicon.

Apple unveiled the iPhone Duo last month alongside the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max. Pre-orders begin October 16, with general availability following a week later.

Supply signals point to stronger-than-expected demand. According to 9To5Mac, citing Securities Times reporting on Apple's supply chain, the company has raised iPhone Duo orders by 30 percent. Total supply for the remainder of 2026 is still expected to land below 10 million units, up from an earlier production range of 6 million to 7 million units.

Apple has also adjusted orders within the iPhone 18 Pro lineup. iPhone 18 Pro Max orders are reportedly up 10 to 15 percent, while iPhone 18 Pro orders have been trimmed by a similar margin. The overall supply target for the Pro series remains unchanged.

That mixed picture contrasts with an earlier Nikkei Asia report suggesting Apple had cut iPhone 18 Pro production due to weaker-than-anticipated demand.

‘No Kings’ protests spread across the US ahead of midterm elections

Thousands rallied across the US against President Donald Trump in ‘No Kings’ demonstrations, a movement that opposes what it sees as Trump’s abuse of power. Demonstrators urged people to vote early ahead of November’s midterm elections.

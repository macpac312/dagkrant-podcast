Ukraine war briefing: Russia building non-negotiable ‘security zone’ inside Ukrainian border, Putin declares

Russian president says area will not be part of any potential peace talks while Kremlin says the zone spans 660km. What we know on day 1,697

Russia is establishing a “security zone” inside Ukraine along the border and it will be non-negotiable in any ⁠future talks with Kyiv, Vladimir Putin has said. The Russian president, wearing military uniform and speaking during a visit to a command post, said the operation ⁠was in response to Ukraine’s ⁠2024-25 incursion into ​Russia’s western Kursk region. “The Russian armed forces were tasked not only with expelling the enemy from our land but also with establishing a security zone ⁠in the adjacent territory. This task is being carried out successfully,” he said in televised comments on Friday. “Our goal is to ensure the absolute security of Russia’s border regions ⁠for the future, and we do not plan to make this a subject for potential peace negotiations with ​Ukraine.”

The planned ‌security zone spanned 660km (410 ‌miles), of which 70% was currently under Russia’s control, Kremlin spokesperson Dmitry Peskov said. Peskov did not say how deep it would ‌reach inside Ukraine. The zone requires Russia to carve out land in the Sumy and Kharkiv regions, which are not among those it is illegally claiming as its own territory.

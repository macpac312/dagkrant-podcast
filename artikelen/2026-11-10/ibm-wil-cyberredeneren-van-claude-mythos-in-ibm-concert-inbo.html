IBM plans to bring Anthropic’s Claude Mythos cyber reasoning into IBM Concert

The planned offering brings together three complementary capabilities:

Scan: Discover what traditional scanners miss.

Claude Mythos would reason across customer-selected application code to help identify previously unknown vulnerabilities, including weaknesses without existing signatures or CVE records.

Prove: Validate what attackers can actually exploit.

Concert Protect’s Proof of Exploitability capability helps teams move beyond severity scores and predicted risk. By analyzing code, configuration, and infrastructure context, it can evaluate viable exploitation paths and provide supporting evidence.

Fix: Accelerate remediation with developer-approved changes.

IBM Concert Protect, together with Red Hat Lightwell, helps withrisk-based vulnerability remediation by identifying and prioritizing exploitable risks, leveraging trusted backported open-source fixes, generating targeted remediation changes, and helping developers to validate, review, and approve fixes through automated pull-request workflows.

Together, these capabilities connect discovery, validation, and remediation in one enterprise-grade platform.

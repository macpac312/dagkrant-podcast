ChatGPT Enterprise Lands on AWS Marketplace

OpenAI made ChatGPT Enterprise available through AWS Marketplace on Monday, letting companies buy the workplace AI suite through their existing AWS billing. The move cuts procurement friction for enterprises and allows eligible spending to count toward AWS commitments.

Anthropic Launches Claude Fable 5.2 With 20% Price Cut Ahead of IPO

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 on Monday, claiming the top spot on 13 of 15 benchmark score lines against GPT-6 Astra and Gemini 4 Carbon. The new models are 30% faster and 20% cheaper than their predecessors, priced at $8 per million input tokens — undercutting OpenAI's flagship. The launch comes days before Anthropic's blockbuster IPO and marks its first frontier release since CEO Dario Amodei called for a slowdown in AI development.

Anthropic Pays $3.4 Billion for 1.7GW of Gas Generators to Power AI Data Centers

Anthropic has spent $3.4 billion to take over a queue position for 1.7 gigawatts of Caterpillar gas generators originally ordered by OnePWR Solutions. It is the largest known single purchase of power-generation equipment by an AI lab, as demand outruns factory capacity for new generator orders.

OpenAI is acquiring Pierre Computer Company, maker of Code Storage and open-source Git tools Trees and Diffs, to transform Codex from an AI coding assistant into a full-spectrum development platform. Claude Code's 'Don't Ask Again' Exposed 2,539 Secrets on GitHub

Security firm Apiiro found that Claude Code's "don't ask again" feature stores passwords, API keys, and SSH logins in plain text in a local settings file — and over 1,000 copies ended up in public GitHub repositories. Anthropic's Claude Code lead acknowledged the issue but existing fixes may not fully solve it.

Revolut Swaps Claude Code for Chinese GLM, Cuts Coding Costs 90%

Revolut has moved roughly 2,000 engineers from Anthropic's Claude Code and OpenAI's Codex to a self-hosted instance of GLM 5.3, an open-weight model from Beijing-based Z.ai, cutting AI-assisted coding costs by approximately 90%. David Abitbol, who leads AI operations and infrastructure at Revolut, shared the migration story at the AI Engineer conference in New York, detailing how the fintech tuned the model from a "terrible" out-of-box experience of 14-second latency down to 1.6 seconds. The move underscores a growing trend: well-funded engineering organizations are betting on self-hosted open-weight models over per-seat SaaS subscriptions for AI coding tools.

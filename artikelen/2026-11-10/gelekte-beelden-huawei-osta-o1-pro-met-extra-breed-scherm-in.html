Huawei Starlight O1 Pro leaks with extra-wide display

Just a few days ago, rumors suggested that Huawei would soon launch a brand-new smartphone series, with the first model expected to have a similar form factor to the Huawei Pura X View. Whether the device will be marketed as the Huawei OSTA O1 Pro, Huawei Starlight O1 Pro or Huawei Starburst O1 Pro remains to be seen, as rumors about the name are still contradictory.

Now, the highly reliable leaker Digital Chat Station has shared an image purportedly showing the front of this smartphone. Unsurprisingly, the front looks almost identical to the Pura X View, as Huawei is likely using the same display, including a punch-hole and relatively thin bezels. The panel apparently has a 16:9 or 15:9 aspect ratio, making the device significantly wider than most smartphones, which use an aspect ratio of around 20:9. Even the back of this Huawei Starlight O1 Pro is said to look similar to the Pura X View, as Huawei is reportedly once again opting for a wide camera module in the style of the Google Pixel 11 Pro ($1,069 on Amazon), although this model is said to have only two rear cameras instead of three.

Huawei is reportedly planning to offer two different models, the Starlight O1 and Starlight O1 Pro, that share the same form factor, but there are likely to be differences in the processor and cameras. With a screen diagonal of 6.39 inches, the O1 series is reportedly relatively compact, although the top model with 16 GB of RAM and 1 TB of storage is unlikely to be cheap. It is not yet known when the Huawei Starlight O1 series will launch.

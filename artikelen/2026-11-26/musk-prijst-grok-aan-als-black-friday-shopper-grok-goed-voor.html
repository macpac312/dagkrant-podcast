Meta already knows how it'll get paid

HTML_TAG_START Boorstin reported that Mark Zuckerberg has said Muse will take a small fee on transactions. SpaceX hasn't said anything like that about Grok. The closest it has is X's ad business, which brought in $538 million in the third quarter, and a bot that knows what you're hunting for on Black Friday holds exactly the data X's advertisers would pay for. Until SpaceX charges a fee or sells ads against those orders, though, the 24% won't show up in its results. HTML_TAG_END

Retailers may make the bots pay

HTML_TAG_START Grok's order share also only matters if retailers let the agents in. Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella said at the DealBook Summit that "if you don't accept the agent, somebody else will. If Amazon doesn't, Shop will." HTML_TAG_END

HTML_TAG_START Walmart may face that choice first. Its U.S. advertising grew 37% in the fiscal third quarter it reported on Nov. 19. So does it welcome Grok and Muse, or charge them for the shoppers its ads depend on? It reports again on Feb. 18. HTML_TAG_END

HTML_TAG_START Shopify is the "somebody else." Its merchants handled $119.1 billion of gross merchandise volume in the third quarter, and if big retailers put up a toll booth, Grok's orders can go to the millions of businesses on Shopify instead. HTML_TAG_END

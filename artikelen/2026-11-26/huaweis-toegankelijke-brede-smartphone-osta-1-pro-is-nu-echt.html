# Huawei’s “accessible” Osta 1 Pro wide-screened smartphone is now a real device

Huawei has made its sub-brand [official](https://www.gsmarena.com/huawei_osta_1_pro_debuts_in_china_-news-75562.php), revealing its Osta 1 Pro and also explaining what ‘OSTA’ means. It’s an acronym for “Original Smart Technology Accessible,” according to company head Richard Yu.

The company has been a little lazy with its first wide-screened device under the Osta label, though. Previous leaks have all [compared it heavily](https://stuff.co.za/2026/10/30/huaweis-rumoured-starlight-o1-pro-wide-bodied-smartphone/) to Huawei’s Pura X View, revealed [earlier this year](https://stuff.co.za/2026/08/21/huaweis-pura-x-view-will-put-a-wider-aspect-smartphone-into-your-palm/). There’s a reason for that.

In terms of specs, the first official Osta smartphone is a clone of the Pura X View. There are a few minor changes to the device, but the main details remain the same. The 6.39in 16:9.5 OLED panel, Kirin 9030S, 200MP main camera, and 7,000mAh battery are all present.

The 1 Pro features up to 16GB of RAM, an uptick from the X View’s 12GB maximum. Huawei’s Osta brand might be giving in this area, but it’s also taking something away. The Osta 1 Pro loses the Pura X View’s ultrawide camera, replacing it with a 50MP 3.7x optical zoom sensor instead. It’ll also drop satellite calling capabilities, which seems to be a sign that the new phone is destined for overseas markets.

However, Huawei hasn’t mentioned any plans to launch it outside of China just yet. If it does achieve escape velocity, it’ll likely arrive at more than the Chinese price of R14,500 for the 12GB/256GB model. Somewhere around the R20,000 mark sounds about right for the South African market, which would still make the Osta 1 Pro competitive with everything else vying for your attention.

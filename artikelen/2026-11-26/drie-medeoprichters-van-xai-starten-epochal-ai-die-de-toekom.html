Ex-xAI Founders Launch Epochal to Teach AI to Predict Future

Three co-founders of Elon Musk's xAI have launched a new startup called Epochal. It aims to build AI that can predict future events. Guodong Zhang, Zihang Dai and Manuel Kroiss announced it on Tuesday.&nbsp;All three left xAI after Musk merged the company with SpaceX in February.

Zhang serves as chief executive. Former staff from xAI, OpenAI, Anthropic and Google DeepMind have also joined the team, according to Bloomberg..What is Time Portal?&nbsp;.Epochal's first product is called Time Portal.

It lets AI agents search and read the web as it looked on a date the user picks. The company says it has indexed more than 1 trillion web pages across 30 years, down to the minute.&nbsp;Epochal says most AI systems learn from the whole internet at once, so they already know how every story ends.

Its approach is different. The model lives through thousands of past days, one day at a time, and tries to guess what happens next without peeking at later events. The firm calls Time Portal a time machine for the internet.It also says the best measure of intelligence is the ability to predict the future.

The company says it wants to help people and firms make better decisions.

Jura S8 Moonlight Silver Automatic Coffee Machine - 64 oz Tank and 4.3 Touchscreen For Specialty Dri

Jura S8 Moonlight Silver is a super automatic espresso machine with a 64 oz water tank, 10 oz bean container and a 4.3" color touchscreen for one touch specialty coffee drinks. For home baristas who want Swiss design and automatic milk drinks, its capacity and programmability matter most.

Jura S8 Moonlight Silver is a fully automatic espresso machine designed for home use that combines a 64 oz water tank, 10 oz bean container and a 4.3" color touchscreen to brew classic espresso and milk based specialties at the touch of a button. With Swiss manufacturing and a compact footprint around 11 in wide, 13.8 in high and about 17.3 in deep, it targets coffee drinkers who want café style drinks without manual grinding, tamping or steaming.

Design, Capacity And Core Specifications

The S8 series sits in Jura's super automatic espresso machine lineup, and the Moonlight Silver color option shares the same technical platform as the Chrome and Piano Black variants. According to Jura's US specifications for the S8, the machine uses a variable brewing unit that handles 5-16 g of coffee per shot, combined with a high performance 15 bar pump and a thermoblock heating system rated at 1450 W, running on 120 V with a 10 A current draw.Jura lists these core specs for the S8.

The water tank capacity for the S8 platform is stated as 64 oz / 1.9 l, which aligns with typical household use where several coffees or milk drinks are prepared per day without refilling.The same source lists a bean container holding 10 oz / 280 g and a coffee grounds container of about 16 servings. Dimensions for the Jura S8 family are documented as roughly 11 in × 13.8 in × 17.6 in (W × H × D), with a weight around 22 lbs, which places the machine in the mid sized countertop segment rather than compact travel coffee makers.Jura's US product page for the S8 Chrome lists 11 in × 13.8 in × 17.6 in and 22 lbs.

A review of the Jura S8 notes that the machine uses a stainless steel conical burr grinder and a brew group rated for a single boiler setup, with pump pressure specified at 15 bar and power consumption again at 1450 W.The same review confirms the 22 lb weight and dimensions around 13.8 x 11.0 x 17.3 in for the S8 family. This combination of burr grinder, integrated brew group and pump puts the S8 in the same hardware class as other mid to high end super automatic machines targeted at serious home coffee drinkers.

Touchscreen, Drink Range And Milk System

Jura equips the S8 Moonlight Silver platform with a 4.3" high resolution color touchscreen display that serves as the primary user interface. On the S8 Piano Black US page, Jura describes the touchscreen as offering 15 different specialties at the touch of a button, including ristretto, espresso, coffee, cappuccino, flat white and latte macchiato, with press and hold functions to access programming for coffee strength, volume and temperature.Jura lists 15 specialties and a 4.3" touchscreen for the S8.

Independent coverage of the Jura S8 highlights its full color touchscreen and notes that the machine offers a range of specialty drinks such as espresso and milk based beverages, with a memo function for user profiles and a two cup function for non milk drinks.This review describes a 4.3 in full color touchscreen and specialty drink options, including milk frother capability. Jura's own specifications list a milk system HP3 / CX3 for the S8 with interchangeable milk spout and programmable milk and milk foam quantities, plus automatic rinsing, cleaning and descaling programs.The S8 specification table includes HP3 milk system, integrated cleaning programs and programmable switch off time.

In terms of cup clearance, Jura specifies a height adjustable coffee spout with a range around 2.8 in to 4.6 in for the original S8, while the updated S8 Chrome US model lists height adjustable coffee spouts and cappuccino spouts with ranges between about 65-111 mm and 107-153 mm respectively.The S8 Piano Black table shows coffee spout height from 72-118 mm (about 2.8-4.6 in)The S8 Chrome data lists cappuccino spout and hot water spout height ranges around 4.2-6 in and 2.6-4.3 in. These ranges define what cup sizes fit under the spouts without moving the drip tray or using a separate pitcher.

It offers the same 64 oz water tank, 10 oz bean hopper and 4.3" touchscreen with 15 specialties, but without the expanded 27 specialty list and P.A.G.2 grinder of S8 Chrome NAB, which is relevant for buyers choosing between keeping the classic S8 platform or paying for newer features.

Use Case, Target Audience And Limits

With a 64 oz water tank and 10 oz bean container, the Jura S8 Moonlight Silver is particularly suited to households where multiple espressos, coffees and milk drinks are prepared daily but the demand does not reach office machine levels. Its 4.3" touchscreen, one touch drink selection and automatic milk system cater to home baristas who prioritize convenience and consistency over manual control of each shot.

The 15 specialty drinks programmed into the original S8, including espresso, coffee, cappuccino, flat white and latte macchiato, cover the core café menu many US households want, while the integrated grinder and brew unit handle dosing between 5-16 g of coffee per shot. For users who prefer more extensive drink customization or integrated Wi-Fi connectivity for app control, the newer S8 Chrome NAB with 27 specialties and P.A.G.2 grinder will be more appealing, but for those who mainly need reliable one touch espresso and standard milk drinks, the Moonlight Silver S8 delivers the hardware profile already documented.

In the wider category of super automatic espresso machines, the Jura S8 Moonlight Silver balances capacity, footprint and price. It is not a budget automatic, given pricing around $2,800-3,299 in the US, yet it remains below Jura's flagship machines and specialized dual boiler prosumer devices. That places it best for home users who are ready to invest in a long term automatic system that is made in Switzerland, offers a touch interface and handles milk drinks automatically, but who do not need the absolute maximum number of specialties or the latest grinder branding.

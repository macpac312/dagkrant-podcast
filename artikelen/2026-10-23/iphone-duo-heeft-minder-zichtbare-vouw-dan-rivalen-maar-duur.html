iPhone Duo's Display Magic: Less Visible Crease Beats Rivals, But Longevity Raises Questions

The battle with screen creases is the main sport for manufacturers of foldable smartphones. The well-known insider UniverseIce published a comparison of four current market players: iPhone Duo, Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8, Huawei Pura X Max and Xiaomi 18 Fold. In the shots, the devices are placed side by side under indirect sunlight, and in these conditions, Apple's solution looks the most advantageous.

Apple Magic or Lighting Features

In the published photos, the crease in the central part of the iPhone Duo display seems less pronounced than its competitors. This is an important point, as the visual perception of the crease directly affects comfort when reading or watching videos. Nuance on 10,000 Folds

Despite the visual advantage "out of the box," there are questions about the durability of iPhone Duo. There was previously information that after 10,000 folding cycles the crease becomes very pronounced. Instead of a barely noticeable line, the user risks getting a "groove", characteristic of the first generations of such devices.

While Apple wins in showcase aesthetics, real operation may introduce its own adjustments. The crease remains the Achilles' heel of the entire category, and no one has yet offered a perfect solution to this problem.

Settlement in euro central bank money is now available: since the launch of Pontes on 21 September 2026, distributed ledger technology (DLT) transactions can be settled in central bank money.

DLT-based assets are being integrated into the collateral framework: since March 2026, DLT-based assets issued in central securities depositories (CSDs) can be mobilised as eligible collateral in Eurosystem credit operations.

Interest from sovereign and supranational issuers is growing: engagement with European sovereign issuers indicates significant interest in exploring tokenised issuance . Eurogroup+ issued a

statement on digital finance (9 July 2026).

Market infrastructures are adapting: European CSDs have launched services for DLT-issued securities that operate alongside existing infrastructure.

The trend is not confined to Europe: SEC staff no-action relief (December 2025) allows DTCC to tokenise existing securities for three years.

## Tokenisation covers the whole financial value chain (2/2)

• Lower issuance costs : fewer intermediaries and manual processes

• Wider SME access : easier entry to capital markets , supporting the savings and investments union (SIU)

• Fractional ownership : a broader investor base

• Atomic delivery vs payment : asset and cash exchanged in an all-or-nothing principle, via smart contracts that are programmable

• Lower risk, shorter cycles : reduced counterparty and settlement risk

• Safe settlement asset : anchored in central bank money, via Pontes

• Programmability : automated coupons, redemptions and corporate actions, via smart contracts

• Collateral mobility : faster, intraday, with smaller liquidity buffers

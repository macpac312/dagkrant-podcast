Xiaomi 18 Fold tops AnTuTu's ranking of the most powerful smartphones for October

The foldable Xiaomi 18 Fold unexpectedly took first place in AnTuTu’s ranking of the most powerful smartphones for October 2026. The device, powered by Xiaomi’s proprietary XRING O3 processor, outperformed flagships based on the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, including the iQOO 16 and HONOR Magic9 Pro Max.

Top three in the ranking

The Xiaomi 18 Fold with 16 GB of RAM and 1 TB of storage scored 4,620,886 points in AnTuTu. Second place went to the iQOO 16, powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, with a result of 4,449,925 points.

HONOR Magic9 Pro Max, running on the same Qualcomm chip, rounded out the top three. Its score reached 4,435,489 points.

As a result, the gap between first and second place exceeded 170,000 points. Xiaomi’s result is especially notable because the smartphone uses the company’s own processor rather than a Qualcomm chip.

Top 10 most powerful smartphones

The top ten includes three Xiaomi models, including the chart leader. Devices from iQOO, HONOR, OPPO, RedMagic, and realme are also represented.

At the same time, AnTuTu scores reflect synthetic benchmark results rather than a universal assessment of a smartphone. The final score is influenced by the processor, graphics subsystem, memory, and software optimization. In real-world use, performance also depends on cooling, workload duration, and specific applications.

Therefore, Xiaomi 18 Fold’s leadership in the benchmark does not by itself mean that it will be the best smartphone in terms of battery life, camera quality, or ease of use.

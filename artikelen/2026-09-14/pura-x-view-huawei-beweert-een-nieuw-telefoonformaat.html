Huawei Pura X View: A Bold New Smartphone Format

Huawei has decided to experiment with smartphone form factors without relying on folding mechanisms. The company has introduced the Pura X View, a model with an unusually wide body and a display boasting a 16:9.5 aspect ratio. The device retains a traditional slab design, meaning no folding or unfolding is required to access a larger workspace.

An Unconventional Body Concept

The key feature of the Pura X View lies in its proportions. While most modern smartphones favor elongated, narrow displays, Huawei has bet on a significantly wider panel. The manufacturer claims this is the first wide-format smartphone of its kind in a classic monolithic form factor.

The device is equipped with a flat 6.39-inch OLED display. Thanks to its unique proportions, it covers 96.1% of the front panel. The bezels on all four sides measure approximately 1mm, pushing the screen as close to the edge as possible. For comparison, the bezels on the iPhone 15 Pro measure about 1.55mm.

Configurations and Pricing

Huawei does not differentiate Pura X View versions by RAM; all models come with 12GB of RAM. The differences lie in the internal storage, which comes in 256GB, 512GB, or 1TB capacities.

The entry-level version is priced at $900, the 512GB model costs $1050, and the 1TB variant is available for $1275.

Initial sales results indicate that the unconventional design has captured significant audience interest. In the first 24 hours, Huawei sold 180,000 units of the Pura X View.

Perhaps the most compelling aspect of Huawei’s experiment here is not the screen itself, but the refusal to follow the obvious path. While the market pivots toward foldables, tablets, and multi-hinge devices, the Pura X View suggests a return to the classic slab—simply making it wider. The success of the initial sales suggests that this idea has a dedicated audience, proving that the future of smartphones may be defined by more than just folding screens; it may be defined by diverse geometry.

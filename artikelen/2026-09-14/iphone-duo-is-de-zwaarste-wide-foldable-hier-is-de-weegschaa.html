JAKARTA - The iPhone Duo weighs 254 grams and is 11.3 millimeters thick when folded, heavier and thicker than a number of folding phones with a wide screen design such as the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8, Huawei Pura X Max, and Xiaomi 18 Fold.

The weight of the iPhone Duo is also higher than the other two high-end iPhone models. The iPhone 18 Pro Max weighs 249 grams, while the iPhone 17 Pro Max weighs 233 grams.

With these numbers, the iPhone Duo is 5 grams heavier than the iPhone 18 Pro Max and 21 grams heavier than the iPhone 17 Pro Max.

The weight difference is also noticeable when the iPhone Duo is compared to a number of other folding phones. The device is only 4 grams heavier than the Pixel 10 Pro Fold and 3 grams heavier than the Pixel 9 Pro Fold.

In everyday use, the weight of the device can feel different when the phone is used in an open state and held with one hand. For thickness, the Galaxy Z Fold 8 is the thinnest device in the comparison of wide folding phones with a size of 9.7 millimeters. Xiaomi 18 Fold is at 10.7 millimeters, while Huawei Pura X Max reaches 11.2 millimeters. The iPhone Duo is slightly above the three with a thickness of 11.3 millimeters.

This comparison shows that the iPhone Duo carries a higher weight and thickness compared to a number of its direct competitors in the wide folding phone category. The difference is mainly visible in weight, with a difference of 53 grams between the iPhone Duo and the Galaxy Z Fold 8.

With a weight of 254 grams, the iPhone Duo also falls into the group of high-weight book-shaped folding phones. Meanwhile, its thickness of 11.3 millimeters puts it slightly above some other wide folding devices that are currently the benchmark.

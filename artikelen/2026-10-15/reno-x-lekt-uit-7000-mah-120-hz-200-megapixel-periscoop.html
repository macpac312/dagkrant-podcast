OPPO Reno X specifications: 7,000 mAh battery, 120Hz display, and 200MP periscope camera.

The OPPO Reno X stands out with its 7,000 mAh battery, 6.35-inch 120Hz display, and both the main sensor and periscope telephoto lens boast a 200MP resolution.

The OPPO Reno X is a wide-bar smartphone whose specifications have recently leaked, attracting attention with its massive 7,000 mAh battery and 200MP dual camera system. Positioned in the mid-range segment, the device boasts many high-end hardware features typically found only on flagship models.

Metal frame design and innovative flat screen aspect ratio.

According to leaked information on social media, the OPPO Reno X features a 6.35-inch flat screen with a smooth 120Hz refresh rate. The biggest design difference lies in the aspect ratio, as the device is expected to use a 16:9 or 16:10 format instead of the currently popular elongated aspect ratios.

For visual comparison, the Huawei Pura X View, in the same segment, currently uses a 5:3 aspect ratio, falling somewhere between 16:9 and 16:10. The Reno X's wide, bar-shaped body design will be reinforced with a sturdy metal frame, providing a solid feel and increased mechanical durability.

The camera system is impressive, featuring a 200MP periscope telephoto sensor.

Besides its striking appearance, the Reno X's photography capabilities are impressive, featuring a high-resolution rear camera system. The device is equipped with a 200MP main sensor integrated with optical image stabilization (OIS), helping to maintain stability when taking photos and videos in low-light conditions.

The optical system also includes a 50MP ultra-wide-angle camera and a periscope telephoto camera with a resolution of up to 200MP. The inclusion of a high-resolution periscope camera in this product promises superior zoom capabilities and high detail, directly competing with leading mobile camera setups.

Dimensity 9000 series performance and a 7,000 mAh battery.

In terms of processing power, the OPPO Reno X is equipped with a MediaTek Dimensity 9000 series processor (specific model number not yet revealed), ensuring smooth performance for everyday tasks as well as demanding graphics applications. Notably, the device integrates a large 7,000 mAh battery, effectively addressing the battery life issue in modern smartphones .

OPPO's segment positioning strategy

OPPO's decision to include high-end features like a 200MP periscope camera and a 7,000 mAh battery in the Reno series instead of the flagship Find series demonstrates a significant strategic shift. The company is expanding its premium experience to the mid-range segment, offering more options that strike a balance between performance, battery life, and mobile photography capabilities.

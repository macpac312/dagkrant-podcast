The international version of the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max has been revealed with 12GB of RAM and Android 17.

The international version of the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max has just appeared on Geekbench AI, revealing some noteworthy information about its software, memory, and AI processing capabilities.

Following their launch in the Chinese market last month, the high-end Xiaomi 18 Pro and Xiaomi 18 Pro Max smartphones are preparing for international release. The latest Geekbench AI test data indicates that the manufacturer is currently testing the devices for the global market.

According to published data, the international version of the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max, with model number 2611FPNFAG, has appeared on Geekbench AI. The device is noted to be running Android 17 and has approximately 12 GB of RAM, based on the memory configuration of the test unit.

Unlike the domestic version which uses the self-developed Xring O3 chip, these international models use the Snapdragon chip. This is also one of the regrettable aspects, given the excellent performance of the Xring O3 chip on the Xiaomi 18 Fold that was recently revealed.

The testing platform only recorded the Qualcomm ARMv8 platform, and did not directly confirm the commercial name of the processor chip. Therefore, the information that the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max uses the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 should still be considered a prediction based on previous reports, and not an independent confirmation from this test.

Notably, the international version of the Xiaomi 18 Pro, with model number 2609BPN61G, also appears on the same platform. This suggests that the company may be testing the software and hardware for both smartphone models before expanding distribution to the international market.

What are the notable points about the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max's Geekbench AI score?

According to test results, the Xiaomi 18 Pro Max achieved Single Precision scores of 2,256, Half Precision scores of 3,556, and Quantized scores of 5,092.

Compared to the international version of the Xiaomi 18 Pro, the Pro Max model scored higher in the Half Precision and Quantized tests, while the Pro version performed slightly better in Single Precision. These results indicate differences in AI processing performance between the two devices for specific task types.

However, this is a specific score for AI tasks, not a typical CPU or GPU performance measurement. Therefore, users still need to wait for more real-world tests to assess the device's gaming, multitasking, and ability to handle demanding tasks.

The opportunity for the global launch of the Xiaomi 18 Pro series.

One of the most interesting pieces of information is the possibility of the Xiaomi 18 Pro and Xiaomi 18 Pro Max being more widely distributed in international markets. Previously, the Xiaomi 17 Pro series was mainly sold in China, making it difficult for users in other markets to access these two high-end versions through official distribution channels.

The appearance of two new models on Geekbench AI signals that testing for the international version may be underway. However, benchmark data does not necessarily mean the products will be available for sale immediately.

There is currently no official information regarding the global launch date, international pricing, or specific RAM and internal storage options for each market. Users should wait for an announcement from Xiaomi to know the exact commercial configuration of the product.

The preparation for an international release of both Pro models represents a strategic shift from the previous generation. In the Xiaomi 17 series, launched in China in September 2025, only the standard version was released globally in early 2026, while the Pro models were only released domestically.

Bringing its high-end models to the global market sooner will help Xiaomi compete directly with rivals like vivo and OPPO. Currently, Xiaomi has not yet announced the official launch date or final commercial specifications.

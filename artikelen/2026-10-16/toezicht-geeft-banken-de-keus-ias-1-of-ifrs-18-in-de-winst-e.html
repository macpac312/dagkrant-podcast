Regulatory reporting on profit and loss: institutions will have option to report under IAS 1 or IFRS 18

The new accounting standard IFRS 18, concerning the presentation of profit and loss items, will come into effect on 1 January 2027, while the EBA’s amended implementing technical standards (ITS)[1] incorporating IFRS 18 are expected to apply only from the September 2027 reference period.

In line with the European Banking Authority (EBA) Opinion published on 8 July 2026, institutions will have the option to report regulatory templates under either the previous (IAS 1) or the new (IFRS 18) accounting standard from January 2027 until the amended ITS begin to apply.[2] This is aimed at alleviating the operational burden on institutions of maintaining two different profit and loss formats during the transition period, in line with the principles of the ECB’s reform agenda: Streamlining supervision, safeguarding resilience.

More specifically, significant institutions within the scope of the EBA’s ITS will be able to use IFRS 18‑aligned FINREP templates as published by the EBA in the technical package for version 4.4 (Phase 1) on a voluntary basis. The same flexibility will also apply to significant institutions subject to the ECB FINREP Regulation[3], which will be amended to reflect changes introduced by the new standard. In the meantime, institutions subject to the ECB FINREP Regulation may refer to the above-mentioned EBA Opinion, together with Annex A or Annex B as applicable, for further guidance.

This option is applicable to less significant institutions only insofar as national competent authorities (NCAs) permit institutions within their jurisdiction to choose between IAS 1 and IFRS 18.

Information regarding the chosen option will be collected from institutions by their NCAs.

An Entirely Plastic-Free Coffee Machine for $100 Off

The world of plastic-free drip coffee makers is small. Caraway recently came out with one that (sadly) isn’t on sale for Black Friday — but there are a couple of models from Simply Good Coffee that (happily) have good discounts right now. Strategist senior writer Michael Zhao first discovered the original model it when he was researching all the ways you could possibly brew coffee without plastic. Now there’s a second-generation version as well as a programmable version for people who don’t mind pre-grinding their beans and want to wake up to a fresh pot. Both have a completely plastic-free (and aluminum-free) brewing path, from the glass water reservoir through the stainless-steel internals and all the way to the carafe, also made from glass.

Though the Simply Good Coffee machines lack the array of color options, they look strikingly similar to the well-designed and much-beloved Technivorm Moccamaster. But Zhao says they’re actually even better at making coffee than the Technivorm thanks to their automatic bloom mode, which has a dedicated button to pre-wet the coffee grounds before brewing begins. Wider circular showerheads promote even extraction, and the metal internals and brew baskets conduct more heat away from the water than plastic parts would, so the brewing temperature ends up being a bit lower than ideal for some lighter roasts. They’re impressive and consistent, he says.

Purchased in June 2021 for 86,500 Yen

I bought De'Longhi's fully automatic coffee machine, the "ECAM35035W," in June 2021. It cost 86,500 yen on Amazon. It was quite a splurge for me at the time, but the bottom line is that as of November 2026, it is still active and well in my living room. I've now been using it for 5 years and 5 months.

Breaking Down the Cost Over Time

86,500 yen divided by 5 years and 5 months comes out to about 16,000 yen per year, or roughly 1,300 yen per month. Buying coffee at a cafe costs 400 to 500 yen a cup, so by this calculation, for the cost of about three cups a month, I've had an environment where I can drink freshly ground coffee at home whenever I want. Even if the initial cost is high, this kind of division really proves its worth for appliances you use for a long time.

Benefits Experienced After 5 Years of Use

You can enjoy freshly ground coffee at the touch of a button, so there's no hassle even on weekday mornings The quintessential "cafe-like" aroma that fills the room when grinding the beans boosts your mood to the max right from the morning You can serve a cafe-worthy cup when guests come over Bean consumption stays consistent, making it easy to plan your shopping

Drawbacks (Honestly Speaking)

The noise is quite loud (the sound of grinding beans). That said, this is unavoidable given that it grinds from whole beans, and since brewing is automatic, it finishes while you're getting ready in the morning, so it doesn't bother me personally. However, family members who don't drink coffee might find it noisy

Summary: Who This Machine Is Perfect For

On the other hand, I think it's over-engineered for someone who only drinks coffee on weekends. My conclusion after five years of use is that it was definitely worth buying.

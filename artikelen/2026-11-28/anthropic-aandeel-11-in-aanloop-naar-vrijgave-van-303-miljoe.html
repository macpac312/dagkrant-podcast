Anthropic (ANT) shares surged 10.98% to $91.88 before slipping 0.51% to $91.41 in after-hours trading. The rally comes ahead of a major share unlock scheduled for December 15, 2026. The upcoming release could expand the company’s publicly available share supply and influence trading activity.

[Anthropic](https://blockonomi.com/anthropic-ant-stock-surges-10-98-as-sp-500-inclusion-faces-delays/) will allow eligible shareholders to sell up to 303 million shares under its first major lockup release. The tranche includes 174 million Class A shares and 129 million Class N shares. Together, these holdings represent approximately $26.9 billion in market value based on recent closing levels.

The artificial intelligence company priced its initial public offering on November 18, 2026. Anthropic raised approximately $81 billion after underwriters fully exercised their additional share purchase option. Subsequently, the company began trading on November 19 under the ticker ANT, gaining 17% during its market debut.

The upcoming release introduces additional supply into a market with relatively limited public holdings. The December unlock arrives just 27 days after Anthropic published its final prospectus. Actual selling volumes will depend on the decisions of eligible shareholders rather than the total number of released shares.

## Anthropic Expands Share Supply Through Staggered Releases

Anthropic adopted a staggered lockup structure instead of the traditional 180-day restriction common among newly listed companies. The arrangement distributes share releases across 23 separate dates, gradually expanding the available market supply. Five tranches through February 2, 2027, collectively cover approximately 1.63 billion shares.

The company’s broader lockup agreement covers roughly 15.5 billion Class A share equivalents. This figure exceeds the total number of shares [Anthropic](https://blockonomi.com/anthropic-stock-falls-below-ipo-price-as-junk-debt-fears-grow/) sold during its public offering by more than 15 times. Subsequent releases could significantly increase the number of shares available for public trading.

Anthropic issued Class N shares to strategic shareholders under a separate ownership structure. These securities convert into Class A shares when holders sell them to unrelated parties. The conversion process allows additional holdings to enter the public market alongside existing Class A shares.

## Index Inclusion Could Support Anthropic Stock Demand

Russell 1000 tracking funds could purchase Anthropic shares at the market close on December 11. Nasdaq-100 tracking funds may follow on December 21, subject to the company’s eligibility and index inclusion. These potential purchases could generate additional institutional demand during the lockup period.

[Anthropic’s](https://blockonomi.com/anthropic-ant-stock-climb-as-kpmg-launches-claude-mythos-cybersecurity-services/) initial public float represents approximately 7% to 8% of its outstanding shares. Nasdaq applies float-based weighting restrictions, potentially limiting the company’s representation within the Nasdaq-100. Therefore, passive fund purchases may remain relatively small compared with the volume of upcoming share releases.

The December unlock will provide an early measure of market demand following Anthropic’s public debut. Subsequent releases will further test the balance between shareholder selling and institutional purchases. Meanwhile, revenue growth, operating expenses and profitability will remain important factors in assessing the company’s valuation.

Banned Nvidia Blackwell chips turn up in a Shanghai data center linked to Alice Huang, Bloomberg reports

It's no surprise at this point, but for the record: Two Chinese companies installed Nvidia's powerful Blackwell chips at a Shanghai data center linked to Alice Huang, who had an affair with Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang. The details come from documents filed with Chinese regulators and reviewed by Bloomberg.

According to the filings, Shanghai Shuoyao offered a GB300-NVL72 rack as collateral for a 96 million yuan ($14 million) loan from Shanghai Innovation Bank, while a second company, Shanghai Kezhan, listed a GB200 server at the same site in loan documents. Exporting these chips to China without a US license is banned. Bloomberg traced the rack to Taiwanese manufacturer Gigabyte through a serial number in a public warranty database.

Shanghai Shuoyao is closely tied to Singapore-based Megaspeed, according to Bloomberg. The Trump administration added Megaspeed to a US trade blacklist this month on suspicion of chip smuggling.

Motorola Razr Flex official-looking images leak ahead of launch

Following the leaks for the Motorola Razr Flex and the more recent image leak, official-looking renders of the phone have surfaced. The Razr Flex is Motorola’s response to the Galaxy Z Fold 8 ($1,550 on Amazon) and the iPhone Duo, sporting a compact folding form factor, though the company has not shared any information about such a device.

SammyGuru was able to uncover what seem to be official-looking renders of the Motorola Razr Flex. They show the compact foldable in different colors and angles. For now, there seem to be two colors, Pantone Dark Botanical Garden and Pantone Coriander. The former has a woven fabric-textured back, while the latter has a leather-like finish.

The images show a design that is in line with the recent leak, as the same dual-camera setup in a raised camera island can be seen at the back. The cover display has a center hole punch for the selfie camera, and the inner display has one in the top right corner. The power and volume buttons are on the right side.

As for specifications, previous leaks have suggested a 4.97-inch cover display, a 6.92-inch main display, a 50 MP main camera, a 50 MP ultrawide camera, and a 5,000 mAh battery. These dimensions suggest the Motorola Razr Flex will be even smaller than both the iPhone Duo and the Galaxy Z Fold 8, and likely cheaper as well. The company is expected to reveal the foldable next month.
